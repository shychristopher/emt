## Read

### Synthesis 1: EMS Architecture, Legal Authority & Public Health (Chapters 1 & 3)
- **System & Oversight:** Modern EMS evolved from funeral-home transport via wartime trauma care, the **1966 White Paper & Highway Safety Act**, **1973 EMS System Act**, **2006 National EMS Scope of Practice Model** (**EMR → EMT → AEMT → Paramedic**), and **EMS Agenda 2050**. States regulate EMS via a **lead state EMS agency** (NHTSA's **10 Standards**), while EMTs practice as designated agents of a physician **Medical Director** under **off-line** (*protocols/standing orders*) and **on-line** (*radio/phone*) direction. **CQI**, **EBM**, **NEMSIS**, **CARES**, and the **8-step cyclical EBG model** drive science-based care.
- **Public Health Integration:** Public health protects populations *before* onset (CDC **5 arms** & **3 Core Functions**; U.S. life expectancy **47.3 yrs [1900] → 78.4 yrs [2018] → 77.0 yrs [2020/22]**). EMTs track **5 SDOH domains** and deliver **Primary** (vaccines, seatbelts/helmets/DUI), **Secondary** (BP checks, reporting abuse, AEDs), and **Tertiary** (acute emergency care) prevention.
- **Scope, Duties, Consent & Liability:** **Scope of practice** (state law: legal) vs. **Standard of care** ("reasonable person": expected quality). **Duty to act** applies on duty and whenever stopping/responding off duty (stay until equal/higher training assumes care). **Good Samaritan laws** (CA **1959**) protect unpaid good-faith care unless **gross negligence**; **sovereign immunity** protects government EMS only.
  - *Consent & Directives:* **Expressed**, **Implied** (emergency doctrine), **Minor** (`<18/21`, 6 emergent conditions, emancipated minor), and **Involuntary** consent. **Living Will** (2 MDs), **Durable POA** (1 MD), **DNR/DNRCC** (physical proof required; cardiac arrest only), **POLST/MOLST** (`<=1 yr`).
  - *Torts, Privacy & Special Rules:* **Negligence** requires all **4 elements** (Duty + Breach + Damages + Proximate Cause). Avoid the **5 intentional torts** (**Abandonment, Assault, Battery, False Imprisonment, Defamation [Slander/Libel]**). **HIPAA (1996)** allows 5 legal disclosures without consent; **COBRA/EMTALA** ("anti-patient-dumping") requires stabilization and scope-matched transfer crews (5 safeguards). Know **accidental hypothermia** exception, **5 presumptive / 4 obvious** death signs, **2022 NASEMSO** 8 biological exclusions, crime-scene preservation, and **Baby Safe-Haven laws** (TX **1999**).

### Synthesis 2: Safety, Wellness, Biomechanics & Packaging (Chapters 2 & 6)
- **Emotional Care & CISM:** Support dying/grieving patients through **Kübler-Ross's 5 stages** (*Denial, Anger, Bargaining, Depression, Acceptance*) without false assurances; **do not clean postmortem crime scenes**. Manage **acute, delayed (PTSD), and cumulative (burnout)** stress via **Defusing** (`<=8 hrs`, `30–45 min`) and **CISD** (**24–72 hrs**, **7 phases**).
- **Infection Control & Scene Safety:** Never act outside your medical responder role. Know the **5 pathogen groups** (bacteria, viruses [COVID-19, Ebola, Zika], fungi, protozoa, helminths) and **OSHA Standard Precautions** (**24-hr** airborne / **48-hr** bloodborne reporting). Use the **6-step CDC hand wash** (`>=20 sec`) or `>=60%` sanitizer, **7-step safe glove removal**, **HEPA/N-95** for TB/COVID-19 (clean-shaven, annual fit test), **Cleaning → Disinfecting → Sterilization** (autoclave), and immunizations. Scout hazmat with binoculars + DOT *ERG*; wear **ANSI/ISEA Class 2/3 or 207 PSV** vests; stage for police on violent/crime scenes.
- **Wellness & Body Mechanics:** Cardio **30–40 min, >=3x/wk** (`Target HR = [((220 - Age) - RHR) × 0.70] + RHR`); **8–10 hrs** sleep (`<24-hr` NASEMSO shifts); PTSD = **6×** suicide risk (**1-800-273-8255**). Apply the **4 principles of body mechanics** (keep close, legs/glutes/abs, stack/never twist, reduce distance); avoid **lordosis/kyphosis**; use **Power Lift & Power Grip** (`>=10 in` hands), **Squat Lift**, or **1-Hand Carry** (`<=45°`); keep heavy carries `<=1 min` and reach `15–20 in`; push over pull.
- **Moves, Devices & Packaging:** Match **Emergency** (long-axis drags), **Urgent** (Rapid/Self-Extrication), and **Nonurgent** moves with devices (**Wheeled 650 lbs, Bariatric 850–1,600 lbs, Portable 350 lbs, Stair Chair 500 lbs [no AMS/spine/leg injury], Scoop 300 lbs [no spine injury], Stokes, Reeves**). Carry `>=3` straps, **feet first down / head first up**, stronger EMT at head + **spotter**; position pregnancy `>20 wks` on **left side / right hip elevated**; use **Neonatal Isolette**; follow all **13 air-medical rules**.

### Synthesis 3: Documentation & Communication (Chapters 4 & 5)
- **PCR Standards & Billing:** *"If it wasn’t written down, it wasn’t done"* / *"If it wasn’t done, don’t write it down."* Record the **U.S. DOT Minimum Data Set** (**8 patient elements** [`BP >3 yrs`, `cap refill <6 yrs`] + **6 admin elements**) with **synchronous clocks**, **>=2 vitals sets**, **pertinent negatives**, and **NEMSIS v3.4** elements (Primary impression = most acute; Routine/Initial/Full vitals; `324 mg` ASA PTA = *"medication already taken"*; procedure attempts = **"1"**). Use **SOAP/CHART/CHEATED**; never invent abbreviations (`PE`); follow the **8-step refusal protocol**; maintain **zero falsification** (**False Claims Act**); correct paper errors with a **single horizontal line + initials** (post-submission adds note, date/time, notify); substantiate medical necessity (**Prudent Layperson**, **Medicare 42 CFR § 410.40(d)**, **Medicaid**, **ET3**).
- **System Radio & Therapeutic Communication:** **EMS Act of 1973** & **FirstNet (2012)**; **Base (80–150 W)**, **Mobile (20–50 W, 10–15 mi)**, **Portable (1–5 W)**, **Repeaters**, **Encoder/Decoder**, **MDT**, **Cellular**, **Telemetry**, **Telemedicine**; **FCC** rules. Follow the **16 radio ground rules** (**1-sec** PTT pause, **2–3 in** mic, **30-sec** pause, plain English, no patient names, **closed-loop echo** repeat-back, *"we"*, *"affirmative/negative"*, *"Over"*), **9 dispatch points**, **24-hr military time**, **SBAR**, oral handoff, and authority transfer phrases. Pair with the **communication loop**, **9 therapeutic responses**, **Three Cs**, nonverbal **distance zones** (`<1½ ft`, `1½–4 ft`, `4–12 ft`, `>=12 ft`), **open/closed** questions (no **leading**), overcoming **ethnocentrism**, and **Family-Centered Care (1987)**.

### Synthesis 4: Anatomy, Lifespan, Terminology & Pathophysiology (Chapters 7 & 8)
- **Anatomical Blueprint & Lifespan:** **Anatomical position**; 7 patient positions (turn recovery `>30m`; Fowler’s `45°–60°`; no Trendelenburg/shock position for shock); 3 planes (**Midsagittal, Frontal/Coronal, Transverse/Horizontal**); 4 quadrants (**RUQ** liver/GB, **LUQ** spleen/stomach, **RLQ** appendix, **LLQ**, **Midline**). **33 vertebrae**, **24 ribs**, **6 joints**, **3 muscle types**, **femur (1–2 L)** & **pelvis (~5 L)** blood loss. Upper airway ends at **cricoid**; **diaphragm (60–70%, `C3–C5`)** + intercostals (30–40%); 4 heart chambers/valves; **RAS + `>=1` hemisphere** = consciousness; **α1** (vasoconstriction/sweat), **β1** (↑ HR/contractility), **β2** (bronchodilation).
  - *Pediatric vs. Geriatric:* **Pediatric** = `4–5 mm` pliable trachea, cricoid narrowest (`<6–8y`), shoulder padding (`<8–9y`), fontanelles (`3m`/`12–18m`), grunting, RR `>=60` inadequate, sudden hypotension after `>30%` blood loss, newborn bradycardia = initial hypoxia sign, `2–3×` metabolic rate. **Geriatric (`65+`)** = valve stenosis, hypertrophy, arteriosclerosis, orthostatic hypotension, no new alveoli `>20`, blunted chemoreceptors, presbycusis, peripheral neuropathy (silent MI), osteoporosis/kyphosis (`2/3`), `1/3` nephron loss (drug toxicity). **Medical Terminology** = Combining form + Prefix + Suffix.
- **Cellular Metabolism & 11 Components of Perfusion:**
  - *Metabolism & Pump:* **Aerobic** (O2 + mitochondria → high ATP + heat/CO2/water) vs. **Anaerobic** (no O2 → **lactic acid** + minimal ATP) → **Na+/K+ pump failure** → Na+ stays in → water follows → cell swells & ruptures.
  - *Respiratory (1–6):* **78.08% N2 / 20.95% O2** (`>=21%`); **CO** (blocks RBCs) vs. **Cyanide** (blocks cells); **Boyle’s law** (`760/758/762/754 mmHg`); Compliance vs. Resistance (edema); `Minute = VT × f` (`6 L/min`) vs. `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`4.2 L/min`, `VD = 150 mL`; low VT needs **PPV**); **Central** (CO2/pH) vs. **Peripheral** (O2 hypoxic drive) chemoreceptors; **VRG (`2s/3s`) / DRG / Pontine**; **V/Q < 1**; O2 (**97–98.5%** Hgb; anemia reads normal SpO2) & CO2 (**70%** bicarb).
  - *Cardiovascular (7–11):* **~70 mL/kg** (`~4.9 L`); **55% Plasma / 45% Formed Elements**; **64% Venous** (drops first in bleeding); **Hydrostatic ("push")** vs. **Oncotic ("pull")**; `CO = HR × SV` (`5 L/min`, **HR >160** drops CO; Preload/Frank-Starling, Contractility, Afterload); `BP = CO × SVR`; **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%`)**; **Precapillary sphincters**; **Baroreceptors & Chemoreceptors** → SNS + epinephrine/norepinephrine.
