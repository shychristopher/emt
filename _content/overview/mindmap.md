# EMT Course Master Mind Map (Chapters 1–8)
- **0. Master Course Bridges (How Chapters 1–8 Interconnect)**
  - **Bridge 1: The Clinical Perfusion Engine (Ch. 1, 6, 7, 8):**
    - Airway, pleural, cardiac, and vascular anatomy (Ch. 7) directly govern Boyle’s law, compliance, resistance, preload, afterload, and SVR (Ch. 8)
    - Primary assessment priorities (Ch. 1) protect the 11 perfusion components (Ch. 8) to sustain aerobic metabolism and prevent lactic acid / Na+/K+ pump failure
    - Patient positioning (Ch. 6 & 7) directly alters physiology (left-lateral pregnancy >20 wks restores IVC preload; Fowler’s aids diaphragm; no Trendelenburg)
  - **Bridge 2: Authority, Consent & Legal Defense (Ch. 1, 3, 4, 5):**
    - State Lead EMS Agency licenses you + Physician Medical Director delegates practice via off-line protocols/standing orders and on-line direction (Ch. 1 & 3)
    - Valid consent/advance directives (Ch. 3) rely on therapeutic communication (Ch. 5) and objective, NEMSIS/DOT-compliant PCR documentation (Ch. 4)
    - Accurate medical terminology (Ch. 7) defends against negligence/torts (Ch. 3), proves medical necessity (Ch. 4), and feeds NEMSIS/CARES/EBGs (Ch. 1)
  - **Bridge 3: Safety, Biomechanics & Longevity (Ch. 1, 2, 6):**
    - Hierarchy of Safety (Self → Crew → Patient → Bystanders) pairs Standard Precautions/PPE/staging (Ch. 2) with 4 body-mechanics principles and power lifting (Ch. 6)
    - Physical fitness (70% target HR), sleep (8–10 hrs, <24-hr shifts), and CISM (defusing/CISD) protect against burnout and PTSD (Ch. 2)

- **1. EMS Systems, Research & Public Health (Ch. 1)**
  - **Evolution & Governance:**
    - Funeral-home origins → military trauma lessons (Korea/Vietnam, Iraq/Afghanistan)
    - **1966** *White Paper* & Highway Safety Act (DOT/NHTSA) → **1973** EMS System Act → **1993** NREMT Blueprint → **1996/2000** *EMS Agendas* → **2005–2006** Core Content, Scope of Practice, IOM *Crossroads* → **2014–2018** *EMS Agenda 2050*
    - **Lead State EMS Agency:** Planning, coordination, regulation, licensing (distinct from NREMT) + NHTSA **10 Assessment Standards** + Gunderson **6 Goals & 12 Service Lines**
  - **911 Access, Models, Tiers & Centers:**
    - **PSAP** + **E-911** (ANI/ALI) + **EMDs**; Wireless (~70%): Basic, Phase I (tower), Phase II (**50–300 m**); **VoIP** (registered address)
    - **6 Models:** Fire, Municipal ("Third Service"), Private, Hospital-Based, Law Enforcement, Public Safety Officer
    - **4 Provider Levels:** **EMR** → **EMT** (pulse ox, auto BP, limited meds) → **AEMT** (advanced airways, BGL, IV/IO, select meds) → **Paramedic**
    - **10 Specialty Centers:** Trauma, Burn, OB, Pediatric, Poison, Stroke, Cardiac, Hyperbaric, Spine, Psychiatric; **MIH-CP** (underserved access, tele-triage, chronic/post-discharge care)
  - **Oversight, QI, Research & Public Health:**
    - Safety #1; lift **125 lbs**; good color vision; **ADA (1990)**; verbal + written report before leaving ED
    - **Medical Director:** **Indirect (Off-Line)** (Protocols & Standing Orders, e.g., chest pain O2, comfort, 1 nitro if SBP > 90, 160–325 mg ASA, ALS) vs. **Direct** (On-Line & On-Scene)
    - **CQI** (6 EMT roles, non-punitive) + **closed-loop communication** + **4-step EBM** + **8-step Scientific Method** + **NEMSIS / CARES / NIGMS** + **8-step cyclical EBG model**
    - **Public Health:** Population focus *before* onset; CDC **5 arms & 3 Core Functions** (Assessment, Policy Development, Assurance); life expectancy **47.3 (1900) → 78.4 (2018) → 77.0 (2020/22)**; **5 SDOH domains**; **Primary, Secondary, Tertiary** prevention

- **2. Workforce Safety & Wellness (Ch. 2)**
  - **Emotional Care, Grief & Stress:**
    - **Kübler-Ross 5 Stages:** Denial, Anger, Bargaining, Depression, Acceptance
    - **9 Dying/Family Principles:** Dignity, respect, clear facts, safe venting, no false assurances; **do not clean postmortem crime scenes**
    - **Stress & CISM:** **Acute**, **Delayed (PTSD)**, **Cumulative (burnout)**; 10 warning signs across 5 domains; **Defusing** (`<=8 hrs`, `30–45 min`) vs. **CISD** (**24–72 hrs**, **7 phases**); 10 CISM components
  - **Infection Control & Scene Hazards:**
    - **5 Pathogen Groups:** Bacteria (antibiotics), Viruses (no antibiotics; EIDs = COVID-19, Ebola, Zika), Fungi, Protozoa (malaria), Helminths (hookworm)
    - **OSHA Standard Precautions:** Airborne exposure (TB) to DO in **24 hrs**; bloodborne (HIV/HBV) in **48 hrs**
    - **Hand Hygiene, PPE & Decon:** **6-step CDC hand wash** (`>=20 sec`) or `>=60%` sanitizer; HEPA/N-95 (clean-shaven, annual fit test); hot wash soiled uniforms `>=25 min`; **Cleaning → Disinfecting → Sterilization** (autoclave); never recap sharps; PPD + COVID-19, Tdap q10y, Hep B (free within **10 days**), flu, MMR, varicella
    - **Hazmat, Rescue & Violence:** Binoculars + DOT *ERG*; **ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2/3** or **207 PSV** vests (`Class 1 <25 mph`, `Class 2 >=25 mph`); stage for police on violent/crime scenes
  - **Wellness & Safe Glove Removal:**
    - **6 Wellness Dimensions** & **5 Fitness Components:** Cardio **30–40 min, >=3x/wk** (`Target HR = [((220 - Age) - RHR) × 0.70] + RHR`)
    - **Sleep & Mental Health:** **8–10 hrs** sleep/day; 5 NASEMSO fatigue guidelines (shifts **<24 hrs**); `>30%` diagnosis; PTSD = **6×** suicide risk (**1-800-273-8255**)
    - **7-Step Glove Removal:** Contaminated-to-contaminated, clean-to-clean only

- **3. Medical, Legal & Ethical Issues (Ch. 3)**
  - **Scope, Duties, Protections & Ethics:**
    - **Scope of Practice** (state law: legal) vs. **Standard of Care** ("reasonable person": expected quality)
    - **Duty to Act:** On-duty; off-duty assumed duty if stopping/responding (stay until equal/higher training); duties to patient, self, partner (report impairment), equipment
    - **Good Samaritan Laws** (CA **1959**; unpaid good-faith unless **gross negligence**); **Sovereign Immunity** (gov EMS only); Statute of limitations; Contributory negligence; **NAEMT Code of Ethics (1978)**
  - **Consent, Advance Directives & Refusals:**
    - **4 Consent Types:** **Expressed**, **Implied** (emergency doctrine), **Minor** (`<18/21`, 6 emergent conditions, emancipated minor), **Involuntary** (incompetent/custody)
    - **Advance Directives:** **Living Will** (2 MDs), **Durable POA** (1 MD), **DNR/DNRCC** (physical proof required; cardiac arrest only), **POLST/MOLST** (`<=1 yr` prognosis)
    - **Refusals:** Medical **capacity** vs. legal **competence**; **7-step refusal protection**
  - **Torts, Privacy & Special Situations:**
    - **Negligence (All 4 Elements):** Duty + Breach (*simple/gross*) + Damages + Proximate Cause (*res ipsa loquitur*, *negligence per se*)
    - **5 Intentional Torts:** Abandonment, Assault, Battery, False Imprisonment, Defamation (**Slander** = spoken, **Libel** = written)
    - **HIPAA (1996)** (5 legal disclosures) & **COBRA/EMTALA** ("anti-patient-dumping", 5 transfer safeguards)
    - **Organ Donors** (patient first), **Accidental Hypothermia** exception, **5 Presumptive / 4 Obvious** death signs, **2022 NASEMSO** 8 biological exclusions, crime-scene evidence rules, **Baby Safe-Haven Laws** (TX **1999**, 3 days–1 yr)

- **4. Documentation (Ch. 4)**
  - **Goals, DOT Minimum Data Set & NEMSIS v3.4:**
    - **3 Goals** (Continuity, Legal, Reimbursement) & **2 Golden Rules** (*written down / done*)
    - **DOT Minimum Data Set:** **8 Patient Elements** (`BP >3 yrs`, `cap refill <6 yrs`) + **6 Admin Elements** + **synchronous clocks**; **>=2 vitals sets**; **pertinent negatives**
    - **2022 NASEMSO / NEMSIS v3.4:** Mandatory timestamps (notified & back in service); **Primary impression** = most acute; **Routine / Initial / Full** vitals; `324 mg` ASA PTA = *"medication already taken"*; procedure attempts = **"1"**; **Mandatory** vs. **Required** (`d`/`e` datasets)
  - **Narratives, Legal & Medical Necessity:**
    - **SOAP/SOAPIE**, **CHART/DCHART**, **CHEATED**; never invent abbreviations (`PE`)
    - **8-Step Refusal**; **Zero Falsification** (**False Claims Act**); single-line paper error correction (post-submission adds note, date/time, notify); ePCR timestamped amendment; **Drop reports**, **MCI triage tags**, **Special reports**
    - **Medical Necessity:** Commercial (**Prudent Layperson**), Medicare (**42 CFR § 410.40(d)**), Medicaid (state), **ET3** (CMS/HHS 2019 / Jan 5, 2020)

- **5. Communication (Ch. 5)**
  - **Hardware, FCC & Radio Protocols:**
    - **1973 EMS Act** & **2012 FirstNet**; **Base (80–150 W)**, **LMRS (700–800 MHz)**, **Mobile (20–50 W, 10–15 mi)**, **Portable (1–5 W)**, **Repeaters**, **Encoder/Decoder**, **MDT**, **Cellular (2–40 sq mi)**, **Telemetry**, **Telemedicine**, **Satellite**; **FCC** rules
    - **16 Radio Rules:** **1-sec** PTT pause, **2–3 in** mic, **30-sec** pause, plain English, no patient names, **closed-loop echo** repeat-back, *"we"*, *"affirmative/negative"*, *"Over"*
    - **AACN**, **9 dispatch points**, **24-hr military time**, **SBAR**, oral handoff report, scene authority transfer phrases
  - **Therapeutic & Nonverbal Communication:**
    - **Sender → Encoding → Receiver → Decoding → Feedback** + **9 Therapeutic Responses** + **Three Cs** (Competence, Confidence, Compassion)
    - **Nonverbal:** Open posture, eye level, **Distance Zones** (`<1½ ft` intimate, `1½–4 ft` personal, `4–12 ft` social, `>=12 ft` public), gestures, eye contact, **haptics**
    - **Open/Closed** vs. **Leading questions**; overcome **ethnocentrism**; elderly, pediatric (truth + eye level), hostile, **Family-Centered Care (1987)**

- **6. Lifting & Moving Patients (Ch. 6)**
  - **Body Mechanics & Lifting:**
    - **4 Principles:** Weight close; legs/hips/glutes/abs; stack/never twist; reduce distance; avoid **lordosis** & **kyphosis**
    - **Power Lift & Power Grip** (`>=10 in` hands), **Squat Lift**, **1-Hand Carry** (`<=45°`); heavy 2-rescuer carry `<=1 min`; 100% effort `6 sec`, 50% `1 min`; reach `15–20 in`; push over pull
  - **Moves, Devices, Stairs & Air Medical:**
    - **Emergency** (Armpit-Forearm, Shirt, Blanket long-axis drags), **Urgent** (Rapid & Self-Extrication), **Nonurgent** (Walking, Direct Ground, Extremity, Direct Carry, Draw Sheet)
    - **Devices:** Wheeled (**650 lbs**), Bariatric (**850–1,600 lbs**), Portable (**350 lbs**), Stair Chair (**500 lbs** powered; no AMS/spine/leg injury), Backboard/Vacuum Mattress, Scoop (**300 lbs**; no spine injury), Stokes, Reeves
    - `>=3` straps; **feet first down / head first up**; stronger EMT at head + **spotter**; Diamond vs. Rectangular; left-lateral pregnancy `>20 wks` (**supine hypotensive syndrome**); **Neonatal Isolette**; **13 Air-Medical Rules**

- **7. Anatomy, Physiology & Medical Terminology (Ch. 7)**
  - **Positions, Planes & Quadrants:** Anatomical position; Supine, Prone, Recovery (turn `>30m`), Fowler’s (`45°–60°`), Semi-Fowler’s (`<45°`), Trendelenburg & Shock (`~12 in`, not for shock); Midsagittal, Frontal/Coronal, Transverse/Horizontal; **RUQ** (liver/GB), **LUQ** (spleen/stomach), **RLQ** (appendix), **LLQ**, **Midline**
  - **Body Systems:**
    - *MSK:* **33 vertebrae** (`7C/12T/5L/5S/4Cx`), **24 ribs**, **6 joints**, **3 muscle types**; **Femur (1–2 L)** & **Pelvis (~5 L)** blood loss
    - *Resp & Cardio:* Upper ends at **cricoid**; **Diaphragm (60–70%, `C3–C5`)** + intercostals (30–40%); 4 heart chambers/valves; SA → AV → His → Purkinje
    - *Neuro, Endo & Visceral:* CNS/PNS + **RAS & `>=1` hemisphere** = consciousness; **α1** (vasoconstriction/sweat), **β1** (↑ HR/contractility), **β2** (bronchodilation); Skin (epidermis, dermis, subQ), GI, Renal, Repro
  - **Lifespan & Terminology:**
    - *Pediatric:* **4–5 mm** pliable trachea, **cricoid** narrowest (`<6–8y`), shoulder padding (`<8–9y`), fontanelles (`3m`/`12–18m`), grunting, RR `>=60` inadequate, sudden hypotension after `>30%` blood loss, newborn bradycardia = initial hypoxia sign, `2–3×` metabolic rate
    - *Geriatric (`65+`, >50% calls by 2030):* Aging `~30`; stenosis, hypertrophy, arteriosclerosis, orthostatic hypotension, no new alveoli `>20`, blunted chemoreceptors, presbycusis, peripheral neuropathy (silent MI), osteoporosis/kyphosis (`2/3`), `1/3` nephron loss (drug toxicity)
    - *Medical Terminology:* Combining form + Prefix + Suffix (`-ac, -al, -ary, -eal, -ic, -ous`)

- **8. Pathophysiology (Ch. 8)**
  - **Cellular Metabolism & Pump Failure:** **Aerobic** (O2 + mitochondria → high ATP + heat/CO2/water) vs. **Anaerobic** (lactic acid + minimal ATP); Low ATP → **Na+/K+ pump fails** → Na+ stays in → water follows → cell swells & ruptures
  - **11 Components of Perfusion:**
    - *Resp (1–6):* **78.08% N2 / 20.95% O2** (`>=21%`); **CO** (blocks RBCs) vs. **Cyanide** (blocks cells); **Boyle’s law** (`760/758/762/754 mmHg`); Compliance vs. Resistance (edema); `Minute = VT × f` (`6 L/min`) vs. `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`4.2 L/min`, `VD = 150 mL`, low VT needs **PPV**); **Central** (CO2/pH) vs. **Peripheral** (O2 hypoxic drive) chemoreceptors; **VRG (`2s/3s`) / DRG / Pontine**; **V/Q < 1**; O2 (**97–98.5%** Hgb; anemia reads normal SpO2) & CO2 (**70%** bicarb)
    - *Cardio (7–11):* **~70 mL/kg** (`~4.9 L`); **55% Plasma / 45% Formed Elements**; **64% Venous** (drops first); **Hydrostatic ("push")** vs. **Oncotic ("pull")**; `CO = HR × SV` (`5 L/min`, **HR >160** drops CO; Preload/Frank-Starling, Contractility, Afterload); `BP = CO × SVR`; **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%`)**; **Precapillary sphincters**; **Baroreceptors & Chemoreceptors** → SNS + epinephrine/norepinephrine
