# Chapter 8: Pathophysiology
- **1. Cellular Metabolism & Sodium–Potassium Pump**
  - **Core Purpose of Emergency Care:**
    - Maintain **perfusion** (continuous delivery of **O2** [catalyst] and **glucose** [fuel] + removal of wastes)
  - **Aerobic vs. Anaerobic Metabolism:**
    - **Glycolysis (Cytosol, No O2 Needed):** Breaks glucose into **pyruvic acid** + small amount of **ATP**
    - **Aerobic Metabolism (With O2, Mitochondria):** Produces **large amount of ATP** + **heat**, **CO2** (exhaled), and **water** (reabsorbed/excreted)
    - **Anaerobic Metabolism (Without O2):** Converts pyruvic acid into **lactic acid** + minimal ATP → acid inactivates enzymes, disrupts cell membranes, causes **cell death**
  - **Sodium–Potassium Pump Failure:**
    - **Na+** = primary extracellular ion; **K+** = primary intracellular ion
    - **ATP** actively pumps **Na+ out** against its gradient so **K+ moves in** (6-step pump cycle)
    - **Pump Failure Cascade:** Poor perfusion → ↓ ATP → pump fails → **Na+ accumulates inside cell** → **water follows Na+** → cell swells → **membrane ruptures & cell dies** → organ failure

- **2. Airway, Ambient Air & Mechanics of Ventilation**
  - **Ambient Air & Toxic Gases:**
    - **Sea-Level Air:** **Nitrogen 78.08%** (`593.4 mmHg`), **Oxygen 20.95%** (`159.2 mmHg`), **Argon 0.93%**, **CO2 0.03%**; ensure `>=21%` O2
    - **FiO2** (spontaneous: room air `0.21`, NRB `~0.95`) vs. **FDO2** (assisted: BVM at 15 L/min `~0.95–0.98`)
    - **Toxic Gases:** O2 displacement (enclosed fire); **Carbon Monoxide** (blocks RBC O2 transport); **Cyanide** (blocks cellular O2 utilization)
  - **Airway Patency:**
    - **Nasopharynx** (obligate nose breathers in infants) • **Oropharynx** (tongue, vomitus, foreign bodies) • **Epiglottis** (swelling/flaccid tongue)
    - **Larynx:** Narrowest at **vocal cords** in adults vs. **cricoid ring** in children **<8 years**; **laryngeal spasm** & laryngeal fracture
    - **Trachea, Carina (`T4` / 2nd ICS), Bronchi & Bronchioles:** **Bronchitis** (mucous inflammation); **Asthma** (inflammation + bronchoconstriction → **wheezing**, **prolonged expiratory phase**, hypoxia, **hypercarbia**)
  - **Ventilation Mechanics & Volumes:**
    - **Boyle’s Law:** Volume inversely proportional to pressure; **Inhalation (active):** diaphragm (**60–70%**) + external intercostals drop pressure `760 → 758 mmHg`; **Exhalation (passive):** rises to `762 mmHg`
    - **Pleural Space ("Water Glass" Effect):** Negative pressure (`754 mmHg`); open chest wound draws air in → lung collapse
    - **Accessory Muscles:** Inhalation (**sternocleidomastoid, scalene, pectoralis minor**); Active exhalation (**abdominals, internal intercostals**)
    - **Compliance** (lung/chest stretch; ↓ in pneumonia, pulmonary edema, flail chest) & **Airway Resistance** (↑ primarily by **edema**, mucus, bronchoconstriction)
    - **Formulas:** `Minute Ventilation = VT × f` (`500 × 12 = 6 L/min`); `Alveolar Ventilation = (VT − VD) × f` (`[500 − 150] × 12 = 4.2 L/min`; `VD ≈ 150 mL`, exhaled air = `17–18%` O2)
    - **4 Causes of Inadequate Ventilation:** Low VT (dead space fills first → needs **PPV**), rate too slow, rate too fast (`>=40/min` adult, `>60/min` peds), ↑ dead space

- **3. Regulation of Ventilation, V/Q Ratio & Gas Transport**
  - **Chemoreceptors & Lung Receptors:**
    - **Central Chemoreceptors (Medulla):** Sensitive to **CO2** and CSF **pH** (*hypercapnic drive*)
    - **Peripheral Chemoreceptors (Aortic Arch & Carotid Bodies):** Sensitive to **arterial O2** drops (*hypoxic drive* in chronic CO2-retaining **COPD**)
    - **Lung Receptors:** **Irritant** (cough/bronchoconstriction), **Stretch** (prevents overinflation), **J-Receptors** (↑ capillary pressure → rapid, shallow breathing)
  - **Brainstem Respiratory Centers:**
    - **Ventral Respiratory Group (VRG, Anterior Medulla):** Motor output to diaphragm/intercostals; **2 sec** on (inhalation) / **3 sec** off (exhalation) = **~12/min**; drives forced breathing
    - **Dorsal Respiratory Group (DRG, Posterior Medulla):** Relays sensory rate/depth input to VRG
    - **Pontine (Pneumotaxic) Center:** Inhibits `VRG-I` to smooth inhalation-to-exhalation transition
  - **V/Q Ratio & Gas Transport:**
    - **Normal V/Q:** **<1** overall (apexes = wasted ventilation; bases = wasted perfusion); **Ventilatory disturbance** (asthma = wasted perfusion) vs. **Perfusion disturbance** (bleeding = wasted ventilation)
    - **Oxygen Transport (~1,000 mL/min):** **97–98.5%** on **hemoglobin** (**4 iron sites**; **oxyhemoglobin** vs. **deoxyhemoglobin**) + **1.5–3%** in plasma *(anemic patients can read normal SpO2 while hypoxic—give O2)*
    - **CO2 Transport:** **70%** as **bicarbonate**, **23%** on **hemoglobin**, **7%** in **plasma**

- **4. Blood Volume, Pump Function, SVR, Microcirculation & BP Reflexes**
  - **Blood Volume & Fluid Pressures:**
    - **Normal Adult Volume:** **~70 mL/kg** (`~4.9 L` in 70-kg adult); **Plasma (55%)** (91% water + albumin, antibodies, fibrinogen) + **Formed Elements (45%)** (RBCs, WBCs, platelets)
    - **Distribution:** **Venous 64%** (drops first in bleeding), **Arterial 13%**, **Pulmonary 9%**, **Capillaries 7%**, **Heart 7%**
    - **Hydrostatic Pressure ("Push")** (↑ → **edema** / **pulmonary edema**) vs. **Plasma Oncotic Pressure ("Pull")** (albumin retains vascular fluid)
  - **Cardiac Output (CO = HR × SV; Resting Adult = 5 L/min, Up to 6×):**
    - **Heart Rate:** SA node automaticity + brainstem cardioexcitatory/cardioinhibitory centers + β1 epinephrine; rates **>160 bpm** cut filling time and **decrease CO**
    - **Stroke Volume (3 Determinants):** **Preload** (**Frank-Starling law**), **Myocardial Contractility** (↓ in CHF, MI, β1-blockers), **Afterload** (aortic resistance; chronic high diastolic BP → LV failure)
  - **SVR, Pulse Pressure & Microcirculation:**
    - **Vasoconstriction** (↑ SVR, ↑ BP, cool skin) vs. **Vasodilation** (↓ SVR, ↓ BP)
    - **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%` of Systolic BP):** Early shock sign (`↓ CO` + compensatory `↑ SVR` → **thready pulse**)
    - **Microcirculation:** Arterioles → metarterioles → true capillaries → venules; **precapillary sphincters** regulated by **local** (temp, hypoxia, acidosis, histamine), **neural** (SNS/PNS), and **hormonal** (α1 epinephrine) factors
  - **Blood Pressure (`BP = CO × SVR`) & Reflexes:**
    - **Baroreceptors (Aortic Arch & Carotid Sinuses):** ↑ BP → ↑ PNS/↓ SNS → ↓ BP; ↓ BP → ↑ SNS/↓ PNS → ↑ HR, ↑ contractility, vasoconstriction → ↑ BP; severe drop triggers **adrenal medulla** (**epinephrine** α1/α2/β1 & **norepinephrine** α1/α2)
    - **Chemoreceptor CV Reflexes:** Triggered by significant **O2 drops** → SNS stimulation (`↑ HR` + vasoconstriction → **pale, cool skin** & **tachycardia**)
