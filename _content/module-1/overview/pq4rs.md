### Module Synthesis 1: Anatomical Language, Structural Systems & Lifespan Variations (Chapter 7)
- **Positions, Planes, Directions & Quadrants:**
  - *Anatomical position:* Standing erect, facing forward, arms at sides, palms forward.
  - *Positions:* **Supine**, **Prone**, **Lateral Recumbent / Recovery** (turn if **>30 min**), **Fowler’s (`45°–60°`)**, **Semi-Fowler’s (`<45°`)**, **Trendelenburg** & **Shock Position (`~12 in`)** (no longer recommended for shock; shock position contraindicated in spine injury).
  - *Planes & Directions:* **Midsagittal** (**midline**, equal R/L), **Frontal/Coronal** (**midaxillary lines**, anterior/posterior), **Transverse/Horizontal** (**transverse line**, superior/inferior). Paired terms: Anterior/Ventral vs. Posterior/Dorsal; Superior vs. Inferior; Medial vs. Lateral; Proximal vs. Distal; Right/Left = **patient's**; Plantar vs. Palmar.
  - *Quadrants:* **RUQ** (majority liver, gallbladder, R kidney, small pancreas), **LUQ** (spleen, stomach, L kidney, majority pancreas, small liver), **RLQ** (appendix, R ureter/ovary/tube), **LLQ** (L ureter/ovary/tube), **Midline** (bladder, uterus, prostate).
- **Musculoskeletal, Nervous, Endocrine & Visceral Systems:**
  - *Skeleton, Joints & Muscles:* **Ligaments** (bone-bone), **Tendons** (muscle-bone). **Skull** (cranium [occipital, 2 parietal, 2 temporal, frontal] + face [13 immovable + 1 movable **mandible**]); **33 vertebrae** (`7 C, 12 T, 5 L, 5 S fused, 4 coccyx fused`); **24 ribs** (`1–7` true, `8–10` false, `11–12` floating) + **sternum** (manubrium, body, xiphoid); **pelvis** (iliac crests, pubis, ischium, **acetabulum**); lower/upper extremities. **6 joint types** (ball-and-socket, hinged, pivot, gliding, saddle, condyloid); **femur fracture = 1,000–2,000 mL**, **pelvic fracture = up to ~5 L**. **Skeletal (voluntary)**, **Smooth (involuntary)**, and **Cardiac (automaticity)**.
  - *Nervous & Endocrine:* **CNS** (cerebrum, cerebellum, brainstem [medulla: respiratory, cardiac, vasomotor centers] + spinal cord + CSF); **PNS** (voluntary vs. autonomic [sympathetic vs. parasympathetic]); consciousness requires intact **RAS** + `>=1` **cerebral hemisphere**. **8 endocrine glands** + adrenergic receptors: **α1** (vasoconstriction → cool/pale + sweat → clammy), **α2** (regulates α1), **β1** (↑ HR, contractility, conduction), **β2** (bronchodilation/vasodilation).
  - *Skin, GI, Renal & Repro:* Skin (**epidermis** [melanin], **dermis**, **subcutaneous**); GI (mouth, esophagus, stomach, pancreas, **liver** [RUQ], **spleen** [LUQ, no digestion], gallbladder, small intestine [duodenum, jejunum, ileum], colon); Urinary (2 kidneys, 2 ureters, bladder, urethra); Reproductive (male/female).
- **Pediatric vs. Geriatric & Medical Terminology:**
  - *Pediatric:* Larger tongue; **4–5 mm** pliable trachea (vs. **20 mm** adult); **obligate nose breathers**; narrowest at **cricoid** until **~6–8 yrs**; shoulder padding until **8–9 yrs** (adult proportions at **10**); posterior fontanelle closes **3 mo**, anterior **12–18 mo**; abdominal breathing, retractions, **grunting**, rate **>=60/min** = inadequate; compensate via **tachycardia/vasoconstriction**, sudden hypotension after **>30%** blood loss; bradycardia = **late** in children, **initial** in **newborns**; O2/glucose consumed **2–3× faster** (rapid **hypoglycemia** & **hypothermia**).
  - *Geriatric (`65+`: 16% → 22% by 2040; >50% EMS calls by 2030):* Aging begins **~age 30**; valve **stenosis**/regurgitation, **cardiac hypertrophy**, **dysrhythmias**, **arteriosclerosis**, blunted **baroreceptors** (**orthostatic hypotension**); no new alveoli after **age 20**, blunted **chemoreceptors**; neuron loss (**mid-20s**), **cataracts/glaucoma/macular degeneration/retinal detachment**, **presbycusis**, **peripheral neuropathy** (silent MI); reduced liver metabolism; **osteoporosis** & **kyphosis** (**2 of 3**); **1/3** nephron loss (**drug toxicity**); thin skin.
  - *Medical Terminology:* **Combining form** (`cardi/o-`) + **Prefix** + **Suffix** (`-ac, -al, -ary, -eal, -ic, -ous` = pertaining to).

### Module Synthesis 2: Cellular Metabolism & Respiratory Pathophysiology (Chapters 7 & 8)
- **Aerobic vs. Anaerobic & Na+/K+ Pump Failure:**
  - *Glycolysis* (cytosol, no O2) breaks glucose into **pyruvic acid** + small ATP. With O2 (**aerobic**, mitochondria), cells produce **large ATP** + heat, CO2, and water. Without O2 (**anaerobic**), pyruvic acid converts to **lactic acid** + minimal ATP, inactivating enzymes and killing cells.
  - *Na+/K+ Pump Failure:* Without ATP, active pumping of **Na+ out** stops → **Na+ accumulates inside** → **water follows Na+** → cell swells → **membrane ruptures and cell dies**.
- **Ambient Air, Airway & Ventilation Mechanics:**
  - *Ambient Air:* **78.08% N2, 20.95% O2, 0.93% Ar, 0.03% CO2** (ensure `>=21%` O2); **FiO2** (spontaneous) vs. **FDO2** (assisted); toxic gases displace O2 (fire), block RBC transport (**CO**), or block cellular O2 use (**cyanide**).
  - *Airway & Pleura:* Upper airway ends at **cricoid**; trachea splits at **carina (`T4` / 2nd ICS)**; **asthma** causes inflammation + bronchoconstriction (**wheezing**, **prolonged expiration**, **hypercarbia**). **Boyle’s law**: **diaphragm (60–70%, `C3–C5`)** + external intercostals drop pressure `760 → 758 mmHg` on inhalation (passive exhalation = `762 mmHg`); negative intrapleural pressure (`754 mmHg`, "water glass" effect) collapses if pleura is breached. Accessory muscles: inhalation (**SCM, scalene, pec minor**); exhalation (**abdominals, internal intercostals**).
  - *Volumes & Control:* `Minute Ventilation = VT × f` (`500 × 12 = 6 L/min`); `Alveolar Ventilation = (VT − VD) × f` (`[500 − 150] × 12 = 4.2 L/min`; `VD ≈ 150 mL`, exhaled O2 = `17–18%`). Low VT requires **PPV** (dead space fills first). **Central chemoreceptors** (medulla: CO2/pH = *hypercapnic drive*) vs. **Peripheral chemoreceptors** (aortic/carotid: O2 = *hypoxic drive* in COPD); **Irritant, Stretch, J-receptors**; **VRG** (**2 sec** on / **3 sec** off = `~12/min`), **DRG**, and **Pontine (Pneumotaxic) center**.
  - *V/Q & Gas Transport:* Normal V/Q is **<1** (apexes = wasted ventilation, bases = wasted perfusion); **asthma** = wasted perfusion, **bleeding** = wasted ventilation. O2 = **97–98.5%** on **hemoglobin** (4 iron sites; **oxyhemoglobin/deoxyhemoglobin**) + **1.5–3%** plasma *(anemic patients read normal SpO2 while hypoxic)*. CO2 = **70%** bicarbonate, **23%** Hgb, **7%** plasma.

### Module Synthesis 3: Cardiovascular Pathophysiology, Hemodynamics & Reflexes (Chapters 7 & 8)
- **Heart, Blood Volume & Fluid Pressures:**
  - *Heart Flow:* **Venae cavae** → **R atrium** → **tricuspid** → **R ventricle** → **pulmonary valve** → **pulmonary arteries** (O2-poor) → lungs → **pulmonary veins** (O2-rich) → **L atrium** → **mitral (bicuspid)** → **L ventricle** → **aortic valve** → **aorta**. Conduction: SA → AV → bundle of His → Purkinje fibers.
  - *Blood Volume & Compartments:* **~70 mL/kg** (`~4.9 L`); **55% Plasma** (91% water + **albumin**, antibodies, **fibrinogen**) + **45% Formed Elements** (RBCs, WBCs, platelets). Distribution: **Venous 64%** (drops first in bleeding), **Arterial 13%**, **Pulmonary 9%**, **Capillaries 7%**, **Heart 7%**.
  - *Hydrostatic vs. Oncotic:* **Hydrostatic pressure ("push")** forces fluid out (↑ → **edema / pulmonary edema**); **plasma oncotic pressure ("pull")** from **albumin** retains vascular fluid.
- **Pump, SVR, Microcirculation & BP Reflexes:**
  - *Cardiac Output (`CO = HR × SV`; Resting = 5 L/min, Up to 6×):* **HR >160 bpm** reduces filling time and **decreases CO**. Three SV determinants: **Preload** (**Frank-Starling law**), **Myocardial Contractility** (↓ in CHF, MI, β1-blockers), and **Afterload** (aortic resistance).
  - *SVR & Pulse Pressure:* **Vasoconstriction** (↑ SVR/BP, cool skin) vs. **Vasodilation** (↓ SVR/BP). **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%` of Systolic BP)** signals dropping CO + compensatory ↑ SVR (**thready pulse**).
  - *Microcirculation & Reflexes:* Arterioles → metarterioles → true capillaries → venules; **precapillary sphincters** regulated by **local**, **neural**, and **hormonal** factors. **`BP = CO × SVR`** regulated by **Baroreceptors** (aortic arch & carotid sinuses → medulla → SNS/PNS + **adrenal medulla** releasing **epinephrine** α1/α2/β1 & **norepinephrine** α1/α2) and **Chemoreceptor CV Reflexes** (O2 drop → **pale, cool skin** & **tachycardia**).

## Review
- **Master High-Yield Numbers, Cutoffs & Formulas (Module 2):**
  - **>30 minutes:** Max time on one side in recovery position before turning.
  - **45°–60° / <45° / ~12 inches:** Fowler’s, Semi-Fowler’s, and Shock position angles/heights.
  - **33 vertebrae (`7 C, 12 T, 5 L, 5 S, 4 Coccyx`) & 24 ribs (`1–7` true, `8–10` false, `11–12` floating).**
  - **1,000–2,000 mL / ~5 L (`~70 mL/kg`):** Femur fracture blood loss vs. total adult blood volume (`4.9 L` in 70-kg adult).
  - **~5 minutes / 60–70% vs. 30–40% / `C3–C5`:** Brain hypoxia onset; diaphragm vs. intercostal effort; phrenic nerve roots.
  - **8–24 (avg 12–20) / 20–22 / 18–37 / 30–60 breaths/min:** Normal respiratory rates for adults, older adults, children, and infants (`>=40/min` adult / `>=60/min` peds = inadequate).
  - **4–5 mm vs. 20 mm / ~6–8 yrs / 8–9 yrs / age 10 / 3 mo & 12–18 mo / >30% / 2–3×:** Pediatric trachea vs. adult; cricoid narrowing; shoulder padding; adult proportions; posterior & anterior fontanelle closures; blood loss threshold before sudden hypotension; pediatric respiratory/metabolic multiplier.
  - **Age 20 / Mid-20s / Age 30 / Age 65+ (`16%` → `22%`, `>50%` EMS calls by 2030) / 2 of 3 / One-third:** Alveolar growth stop (20); neuron loss onset (mid-20s); normal aging onset (30); geriatric demographics; elderly kyphosis prevalence (2/3); nephron/kidney weight loss (1/3).
  - **78.08% N2 / 20.95% O2 / 0.93% Ar / 0.03% CO2:** Sea-level ambient air (ensure `>=21%` O2; exhaled air = `17–18%` O2).
  - **760 / 758 / 762 / 754 mmHg:** Atmospheric, inspiratory, expiratory, and intrapleural pressures.
  - **Minute Ventilation:** `VT × f` (`500 × 12 = 6 L/min`).
  - **Alveolar Ventilation:** `(VT − VD) × f` (`[500 − 150] × 12 = 4.2 L/min`).
  - **2 sec on / 3 sec off (`~12/min`):** Normal VRG respiratory rhythm cycle.
  - **97–98.5% / 1.5–3% & 70% / 23% / 7%:** O2 transport (Hgb vs. plasma) and CO2 transport (bicarbonate vs. Hgb vs. plasma).
  - **Blood Composition & Distribution:** **55% Plasma** (91% water) and **45% Formed Elements**; **64%** Venous, **13%** Arterial, **9%** Pulmonary, **7%** Capillaries, **7%** Heart.
  - **Cardiac Output:** `CO = HR × SV` (`5 L/min` resting, up to `6×`; **HR >160 bpm** decreases CO).
  - **Blood Pressure:** `BP = CO × SVR`.
  - **Narrow Pulse Pressure:** `<25%` of Systolic BP.
