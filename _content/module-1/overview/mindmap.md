# Module 1: Anatomy, Physiology, Medical Terminology & Pathophysiology
- **1. Anatomical Terms, Positions, Planes & Quadrants (Ch. 7)**
  - **Foundations:** **Anatomy** (structure) vs. **Physiology** (function); **Anatomical Position** (erect, facing forward, arms at sides, palms forward)
  - **Patient Positions:** **Supine**, **Prone**, **Lateral Recumbent / Recovery** (turn if `>30 min`), **Fowler’s (`45°–60°`)**, **Semi-Fowler’s (`<45°`)**, **Trendelenburg** & **Shock Position (`~12 in`)** (no longer recommended for shock; shock position contraindicated in spine injury)
  - **Planes & Lines:** **Sagittal / Median** & **Midsagittal** (**Midline**); **Frontal / Coronal** (**Midaxillary lines**, anterior/posterior); **Transverse / Horizontal / Axial** (**Transverse line**, superior/inferior)
  - **Directions:** Anterior/Ventral vs. Posterior/Dorsal; Superior vs. Inferior; Medial vs. Lateral; Proximal vs. Distal; Right/Left = **patient's**; Bilateral/Unilateral/Ipsilateral/Contralateral; Midclavicular, Midaxillary, Plantar, Palmar
  - **Abdominal Quadrants:** **RUQ** (majority liver, gallbladder, R kidney, colon, small pancreas), **LUQ** (spleen, stomach, L kidney, majority pancreas, small liver), **RLQ** (appendix, R ureter/ovary/tube), **LLQ** (L ureter/ovary/tube), **Midline** (bladder, uterus, prostate)

- **2. Musculoskeletal, Nervous, Endocrine & Visceral Systems (Ch. 7)**
  - **Musculoskeletal:** **Ligaments** (bone-bone) & **Tendons** (muscle-bone); 4 skeletal functions (shape, protection, motion, mineral storage/RBCs)
    - *Axial:* **Skull** (**Cranium**: occipital, 2 parietal, 2 temporal, frontal; **Face**: 13 immovable + 1 movable **mandible**); **33 Vertebrae** (`C1–C7`, `T1–T12`, `L1–L5`, `S1–S5` fused, 4 fused **coccyx**); **Thorax** (**24 ribs**: 1–7 true, 8–10 false, 11–12 floating + **sternum**: manubrium, body, xiphoid)
    - *Appendicular:* **Pelvis** (iliac crests, pubis, ischium, **acetabulum**); **Lower** (femur, patella, tibia [**medial malleolus**], fibula [**lateral malleolus**], calcaneus, 5 metatarsals, 14 toe phalanges); **Upper** (clavicle, scapula [**acromion**], humerus, radius, ulna [**olecranon**], 8 carpals, metacarpals, phalanges)
    - *Joints, Motions & Hemorrhage:* **Ball-and-socket, Hinged, Pivot, Gliding, Saddle, Condyloid**; Flexion/Extension, Abduction/Adduction, Circumduction, Pronation/Supination; **Femur = 1,000–2,000 mL**, **Pelvis = up to ~5 L**; **Skeletal (voluntary), Smooth (involuntary), Cardiac (automaticity)**
  - **Nervous System:** **CNS** (**Cerebrum**, **Cerebellum**, **Brainstem** [medulla: respiratory, cardiac, vasomotor centers] + spinal cord + CSF); **PNS** (afferent/efferent; **Voluntary** vs. **Autonomic** [sympathetic vs. parasympathetic]); Consciousness = **RAS** + `>=1` **cerebral hemisphere**
  - **Endocrine & Receptors:** Pineal, Pituitary, Thyroid, Parathyroid, Adrenals, Pancreas Islets (insulin/glucagon), Gonads, Thymus; **α1** (vasoconstriction → cool/pale + sweat → clammy), **α2** (regulates α1), **β1** (↑ HR/contractility/conduction), **β2** (bronchodilation/vasodilation)
  - **Integumentary, GI, Renal & Repro:** **Skin** (**Epidermis** [melanin], **Dermis**, **Subcutaneous**); **GI** (mouth, esophagus, stomach, pancreas, **liver** [RUQ], **spleen** [LUQ, no digestion], gallbladder, small intestine [duodenum/jejunum/ileum], colon); **Urinary** (2 kidneys, 2 ureters, bladder, urethra); **Reproductive** (male/female)

- **3. Pediatric, Geriatric & Medical Terminology (Ch. 7)**
  - **Pediatric Differences:** Larger tongue; **4–5 mm** pliable trachea (vs. **20 mm** adult); **obligate nose breathers**; narrowest at **cricoid** until **~6–8 yrs**; higher epiglottis; shoulder padding until **8–9 yrs** (adult proportions at **10**); posterior fontanelle closes **3 mo**, anterior **12–18 mo**; abdominal breathing, retractions, **grunting**, rate **>=60/min** = inadequate; compensate via **tachycardia/vasoconstriction**, sudden hypotension after **>30%** blood loss; bradycardia = **late** in children, **initial** in **newborns**; O2/glucose consumed **2–3× faster** (rapid **hypoglycemia** & **hypothermia**)
  - **Geriatric Changes (`65+`: 16% → 22% by 2040; >50% EMS calls by 2030):** Aging begins **~age 30**; valve **stenosis**/regurgitation, **cardiac hypertrophy**, fewer pacemakers, **dysrhythmias**, **arteriosclerosis**, blunted **baroreceptors** (**orthostatic hypotension**); no new alveoli after **age 20**, blunted **chemoreceptors**, weaker cough/cilia; neuron loss (**mid-20s**), **cataracts/glaucoma/macular degeneration/retinal detachment**, **presbycusis**, **peripheral neuropathy** (silent MI); reduced liver metabolism; **osteoporosis** & **kyphosis** (**2 of 3**); **1/3** nephron loss (**drug toxicity**); thin skin
  - **Medical Terminology:** **Combining form** (`cardi/o-`) + **Prefix** + **Suffix** (`-ac, -al, -ary, -eal, -ic, -ous` = pertaining to); e.g., `hypo-vol/o-emic`, `hyper-glyc/o-emia`, `a-pne/o-a`, `tachy-cardi/o-ia`, `brady-pne/o-a`, `laryng/o-trache/o-bronch/o-itis`

- **4. Cellular Metabolism & Respiratory Pathophysiology (Ch. 8)**
  - **Metabolism & Na+/K+ Pump:**
    - **Glycolysis** (cytosol, no O2 → pyruvic acid + small ATP) → **Aerobic** (mitochondria + O2 → **large ATP** + heat, CO2, water) vs. **Anaerobic** (no O2 → **lactic acid** + minimal ATP → enzyme failure & **cell death**)
    - **Na+/K+ Pump Failure:** Poor perfusion → ↓ ATP → pump fails → **Na+ stays inside** → **water follows Na+** → cell swells → **membrane ruptures & cell dies**
  - **Air, Airway & Ventilation Mechanics:**
    - **Ambient Air:** **78.08% N2, 20.95% O2, 0.93% Ar, 0.03% CO2** (`>=21%` O2); **FiO2** vs. **FDO2**; Toxic gases: O2 displacement, **CO** (blocks RBC transport), **Cyanide** (blocks cell O2 use)
    - **Airway & Asthma:** Upper airway ends at **cricoid**; **carina (`T4` / 2nd ICS)**; **asthma** = inflammation + bronchoconstriction → **wheezing**, **prolonged expiration**, **hypercarbia**
    - **Boyle’s Law & Pleura:** Inhalation (**diaphragm 60–70%** + external intercostals: `760 → 758 mmHg`); Exhalation (`762 mmHg`); Pleural negative pressure (`754 mmHg`, "water glass" effect); Accessory muscles (inhalation: **SCM, scalene, pec minor**; exhalation: **abdominals, internal intercostals**); **Compliance** vs. **Airway Resistance** (↑ by **edema**)
    - **Ventilation Formulas:** `Minute = VT × f` (`500 × 12 = 6 L/min`); `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`[500 − 150] × 12 = 4.2 L/min`; `VD ≈ 150 mL`, exhaled O2 = `17–18%`); low VT needs **PPV**; 4 causes of inadequate ventilation (`>=40/min` adult, `>60/min` peds)
  - **Breathing Control, V/Q & Gas Transport:**
    - **Chemoreceptors & Lung Receptors:** **Central** (medulla: CO2/pH = *hypercapnic drive*); **Peripheral** (aortic/carotid: O2 = *hypoxic drive* in COPD); **Irritant**, **Stretch**, **J-Receptors**
    - **Brainstem Centers:** **VRG** (motor: **2 sec** on / **3 sec** off = `~12/min`), **DRG** (sensory relay), **Pontine/Pneumotaxic** (inhibits `VRG-I`)
    - **V/Q Ratio (`<1` normal):** Apexes = wasted ventilation; Bases = wasted perfusion; Ventilatory mismatch (asthma) vs. Perfusion mismatch (bleeding)
    - **Gas Transport:** O2 = **97–98.5%** on **hemoglobin** (4 iron sites; **oxyhemoglobin/deoxyhemoglobin**) + **1.5–3%** plasma *(anemia reads normal SpO2 while hypoxic)*; CO2 = **70%** bicarbonate, **23%** Hgb, **7%** plasma

- **5. Cardiovascular Pathophysiology, Hemodynamics & Reflexes (Ch. 8)**
  - **Blood Volume & Fluid Pressures:**
    - **~70 mL/kg** (`~4.9 L`); **55% Plasma** (91% water + **albumin**, antibodies, **fibrinogen**) + **45% Formed Elements** (RBCs, WBCs, platelets)
    - **Distribution:** **Venous 64%** (drops first in bleeding), **Arterial 13%**, **Pulmonary 9%**, **Capillaries 7%**, **Heart 7%**
    - **Hydrostatic Pressure ("Push")** (↑ → **edema / pulmonary edema**) vs. **Plasma Oncotic Pressure ("Pull")** (**albumin**)
  - **Pump, SVR, Microcirculation & BP Reflexes:**
    - **Cardiac Output (`CO = HR × SV`; Resting = 5 L/min, Up to 6×):** **HR >160 bpm** cuts filling time and decreases CO; **3 SV Determinants:** **Preload** (**Frank-Starling law**), **Contractility**, **Afterload**
    - **SVR & Pulse Pressure:** **Vasoconstriction** (↑ SVR/BP, cool skin) vs. **Vasodilation**; **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%` of Systolic BP)** = early shock (`↓ CO` + `↑ SVR` → **thready pulse**)
    - **Microcirculation:** Arterioles → metarterioles → true capillaries → venules; **precapillary sphincters** regulated by **local**, **neural**, and **hormonal** factors
    - **BP (`BP = CO × SVR`) & Reflexes:** **Baroreceptors** (aortic arch & carotid sinuses) signal medulla → SNS/PNS + **adrenal medulla** (**epinephrine** α1/α2/β1 & **norepinephrine** α1/α2); **Chemoreceptor CV Reflexes** (O2 drop → **pale, cool skin** & **tachycardia**)
