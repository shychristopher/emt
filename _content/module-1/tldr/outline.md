## The Big Picture: How Module 1 Fits Together
- **Chapter 7 (Structure, Function, Lifespan & Language):** Gives you the map of the body—standardized positions, planes, directional terms, all 9 body systems, pediatric and geriatric differences, and medical word parts.

## Chapter 7: Anatomy, Physiology, and Medical Terminology
- **Positions, Planes & Quadrants:**
  - **Anatomical Position:** Standing erect, facing forward, arms at sides, palms forward.
  - **Positions:** **Supine** (face up), **Prone** (face down), **Lateral Recumbent / Recovery** (turn if `>30 min`), **Fowler’s (`45°–60°`)**, **Semi-Fowler’s (`<45°`)**, **Trendelenburg** & **Shock Position (`~12 in`)** (no longer recommended for shock; shock position contraindicated in spine injury).
  - **Planes & Directions:** **Midsagittal** (equal R/L), **Frontal / Coronal** (anterior/posterior), **Transverse / Horizontal** (superior/inferior). Right/Left always refer to the **patient**.
  - **Quadrants:** **RUQ** (majority liver, gallbladder, R kidney), **LUQ** (spleen, stomach, L kidney, majority pancreas), **RLQ** (appendix, R ureter/ovary/tube), **LLQ** (L ureter/ovary/tube), **Midline** (bladder, uterus, prostate).
- **Core Body Systems:**
  - **Musculoskeletal:** **Ligaments** (bone-to-bone), **tendons** (muscle-to-bone); **33 vertebrae** (`7 C, 12 T, 5 L, 5 S fused, 4 coccyx fused`); **24 ribs** (`1–7` true, `8–10` false, `11–12` floating); **6 joint types** (ball-and-socket, hinged, pivot, gliding, saddle, condyloid); **3 muscle types** (skeletal, smooth, cardiac [**automaticity**]); **femur fracture** loses `1,000–2,000 mL`, **pelvic fracture** up to `~5 L`.
  - **Respiratory:** Upper airway ends at **cricoid cartilage**; lower airway = trachea (**15–20** C-rings), bronchi, bronchioles, **alveoli** (**surfactant**); **diaphragm** (**60–70%**, `C3–C5`) + **intercostals** (**30–40%**); normal rates: **Adults** `12–20` (`8–24`), **Children** `18–37`, **Infants** `30–60`.
  - **Circulatory:** **Venae cavae** → R atrium → **tricuspid** → R ventricle → **pulmonary valve** → pulmonary arteries → lungs → pulmonary veins → L atrium → **mitral (bicuspid)** → L ventricle → **aortic valve** → **aorta**; SA → AV → His → Purkinje; O2 = **97%** hemoglobin / **3%** plasma; CO2 = **70%** bicarbonate / **23%** Hgb / **7%** plasma.
  - **Nervous & Endocrine:** **CNS** (cerebrum, cerebellum, brainstem [medulla: respiratory, cardiac, vasomotor centers]) + **PNS** (voluntary vs. autonomic [sympathetic vs. parasympathetic]); consciousness requires intact **RAS** + `>=1` **cerebral hemisphere**. Adrenergic receptors: **α1** (vasoconstriction → cool/pale + sweat → clammy), **α2** (regulates α1), **β1** (↑ HR/contractility/conduction), **β2** (bronchodilation/vasodilation).
  - **Visceral & Skin:** Skin (**epidermis, dermis, subcutaneous**); GI (mouth, esophagus, stomach, pancreas, **liver** [RUQ], **spleen** [LUQ, no digestion], gallbladder, small/large intestine); Urinary (2 kidneys, 2 ureters, bladder, urethra); Reproductive.
- **Lifespan Differences & Medical Terminology:**
  - **Pediatric:** Larger tongue, **4–5 mm** pliable trachea, **obligate nose breathers**, narrowest at **cricoid** (`<6–8 yrs`), large occiput (shoulder padding until `8–9 yrs`), fontanelles close at **3 mo** (posterior) & **12–18 mo** (anterior), abdominal breathing/grunting, rate `>=60/min` = inadequate, compensate via **tachycardia/vasoconstriction** until **>30%** blood loss, bradycardia = **initial** hypoxia sign in newborns (**late** in children), **2–3×** metabolic rate (rapid **hypoglycemia** & **hypothermia**).
  - **Geriatric (`65+`, >50% of EMS calls by 2030):** Aging begins **~age 30**; valve **stenosis**/regurgitation, **cardiac hypertrophy**, **dysrhythmias**, **arteriosclerosis**, blunted **baroreceptors** (**orthostatic hypotension**), no new alveoli after **age 20**, blunted **chemoreceptors**, **presbycusis**, **peripheral neuropathy** (silent MI), **osteoporosis** & **kyphosis** (**2 of 3**), **1/3** nephron loss (**drug toxicity**), thin skin.
  - **Medical Terminology:** **Combining form** (`cardi/o-`) + **Prefix** + **Suffix** (`-ac, -al, -ary, -eal, -ic, -ous` = pertaining to).

## Chapter 8: Pathophysiology
- **Cellular Metabolism & Na+/K+ Pump Failure:**
  - **Aerobic (With O2):** Glycolysis → mitochondria → **large ATP** + heat, CO2, water.
  - **Anaerobic (Without O2):** Glycolysis → **lactic acid** + minimal ATP → enzyme failure & **cell death**.
  - **Na+/K+ Pump Failure:** Low ATP stops active pumping of **Na+ out** → **Na+ accumulates inside** → **water follows Na+** → cell swells → **membrane ruptures & cell dies**.
- **Respiratory Perfusion Components (1–6):**
  - **Ambient Air:** **78.08% N2, 20.95% O2, 0.93% Ar, 0.03% CO2** (`>=21%` O2); **FiO2** vs. **FDO2**; **CO** blocks RBC transport, **cyanide** blocks cellular O2 use.
  - **Airway & Mechanics:** **Carina** (`T4`/2nd ICS); **asthma** = inflammation + bronchoconstriction (**wheezing**, **prolonged expiration**, **hypercarbia**). **Boyle’s law**: inhalation drops pressure `760 → 758 mmHg` (exhalation `762 mmHg`; pleural space `754 mmHg`, "water glass" effect). **Compliance** (lung/chest stretch) vs. **Airway resistance** (↑ by **edema**).
  - **Ventilation Volumes:** `Minute = VT × f` (`500 × 12 = 6 L/min`); `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`[500 − 150] × 12 = 4.2 L/min`; `VD ≈ 150 mL`). Low VT starves alveoli because dead space fills first → needs **PPV**.
  - **Control, V/Q & Gas Transport:** **Central chemoreceptors** (medulla: CO2/pH = *hypercapnic drive*) vs. **Peripheral** (aortic/carotid: O2 = *hypoxic drive* in COPD); **VRG** (`2s on / 3s off = ~12/min`), **DRG**, **Pontine center**; **Irritant, Stretch, J-receptors**. Normal **V/Q < 1** (asthma = wasted perfusion; bleeding = wasted ventilation). **Anemic patients** can read normal SpO2 while hypoxic—give O2.
- **Cardiovascular Perfusion Components (7–11):**
  - **Blood Volume & Pressures:** **~70 mL/kg** (`~4.9 L`); **55% Plasma** (91% water + **albumin**) / **45% Formed Elements**; **64% Venous** (drops first in bleeding), 13% Arterial, 9% Pulmonary, 7% Capillaries, 7% Heart. **Hydrostatic ("push")** causes **edema**; **Plasma Oncotic ("pull")** retains fluid.
  - **Pump, SVR, Microcirculation & Reflexes:** **`CO = HR × SV`** (resting `5 L/min`, up to `6×`; **HR >160 bpm** decreases CO; SV = **Preload [Frank-Starling]**, **Contractility**, **Afterload**). **`BP = CO × SVR`**; **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%` of systolic BP)** = early shock (`↓ CO` + `↑ SVR` → **thready pulse**). **Precapillary sphincters** regulated by local, neural, and hormonal factors. **Baroreceptors** (aortic/carotid) and **Chemoreceptors** trigger SNS + **adrenal medulla** (**epinephrine/norepinephrine**) to restore BP.
