# Module 1: A&P, Medical Terminology & Pathophysiology — 5-Mile-High TL;DR
- **1. Anatomical Language & Body Systems (Ch. 7)**
  - **Positions & Planes:** Anatomical position; Supine, Prone, Recovery (turn `>30m`), Fowler’s (`45°–60°`), Semi-Fowler’s (`<45°`), Trendelenburg & Shock (`~12 in`, not for shock); Midsagittal, Frontal/Coronal, Transverse/Horizontal; RUQ (liver/GB), LUQ (spleen/stomach), RLQ (appendix), LLQ, Midline
  - **Musculoskeletal:** Ligaments (bone-bone), Tendons (muscle-bone); 33 vertebrae (`7C/12T/5L/5S/4Coccyx`), 24 ribs (`1–7` true, `8–10` false, `11–12` floating); 6 joint types; 3 muscle types (Skeletal, Smooth, Cardiac [automaticity]); Femur (`1–2 L`) & Pelvis (`~5 L`) hemorrhage
  - **Respiratory & Circulatory:** Upper ends at cricoid; Diaphragm (`60–70%`, `C3–C5`) + Intercostals (`30–40%`); Normal RR (Adult `12–20`, Child `18–37`, Infant `30–60`); 4 heart chambers/valves (Tricuspid, Pulmonary, Mitral, Aortic); SA → AV → His → Purkinje; O2 (`97%` Hgb) & CO2 (`70%` bicarb)
  - **Nervous, Endocrine & Visceral:** CNS/PNS + RAS & `>=1` hemisphere = consciousness; α1 (vasoconstriction/sweat), β1 (↑ HR/contractility), β2 (bronchodilation); Skin (epidermis, dermis, subQ), GI, Renal, Reproductive

- **2. Lifespan Differences & Medical Terminology (Ch. 7)**
  - **Pediatric:** Larger tongue, `4–5 mm` pliable trachea, obligate nose breathers, cricoid narrowest (`<6–8y`), shoulder padding (`<8–9y`), fontanelles (`3m` / `12–18m`), abdominal breathing/grunting, RR `>=60` inadequate, sudden hypotension after `>30%` blood loss, newborn bradycardia = initial hypoxia sign, `2–3×` metabolic rate (hypoglycemia/hypothermia)
  - **Geriatric (`65+`, >50% EMS calls by 2030):** Aging starts `~30`; valve stenosis, hypertrophy, arteriosclerosis, orthostatic hypotension, no new alveoli `>20`, blunted chemoreceptors, presbycusis, peripheral neuropathy (silent MI), osteoporosis & kyphosis (`2/3`), `1/3` nephron loss (drug toxicity), thin skin
  - **Medical Terminology:** Combining form (`cardi/o-`) + Prefix + Suffix (`-ac, -al, -ary, -eal, -ic, -ous` = pertaining to)

- **3. Cellular Metabolism & Respiratory Pathophysiology (Ch. 8)**
  - **Metabolism & Pump Failure:** Aerobic (O2 + mitochondria = high ATP + heat/CO2/water) vs. Anaerobic (lactic acid + minimal ATP); Low ATP → Na+/K+ pump fails → Na+ stays in → water follows → cell swells & ruptures
  - **Air, Airway & Mechanics:** `78%` N2 / `21%` O2; CO (blocks RBCs) vs. Cyanide (blocks cells); Boyle’s law (`760/758/762/754 mmHg`); Compliance vs. Resistance (edema); `Minute = VT × f` (`6 L/min`) vs. `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`4.2 L/min`, `VD = 150 mL`); Low VT needs PPV
  - **Control, V/Q & Transport:** Central (CO2/pH) vs. Peripheral (O2 hypoxic drive in COPD); VRG (`2s/3s`), DRG, Pontine; Normal V/Q `<1` (asthma = wasted perfusion, bleeding = wasted ventilation); Anemic patients read normal SpO2 while hypoxic

- **4. Cardiovascular Pathophysiology & Reflexes (Ch. 8)**
  - **Blood & Fluid Pressures:** `~70 mL/kg` (`~4.9 L`); `55%` Plasma (`91%` water, albumin) / `45%` Formed Elements; `64%` Venous (drops first in bleeding); Hydrostatic ("push" → edema) vs. Oncotic ("pull" → albumin)
  - **Pump, SVR & BP Reflexes:** `CO = HR × SV` (`5 L/min`, HR `>160` drops CO; Preload/Frank-Starling, Contractility, Afterload); `BP = CO × SVR`; Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%` systolic = early shock/thready pulse); Precapillary sphincters (local, neural, hormonal); Baroreceptors & Chemoreceptors → SNS + epinephrine/norepinephrine
