## Read

### 1. Structural Anatomy, Lifespan & Terminology (Chapter 7)
- **Language & Landmarks:** **Anatomical position** (erect, facing forward, palms forward). Positions: **Supine, Prone, Recovery** (turn `>30m`), **Fowler’s (`45°–60°`), Semi-Fowler’s (`<45°`)**, **Trendelenburg & Shock (`~12 in`)** (no longer recommended for shock). Planes: **Midsagittal** (equal R/L), **Frontal/Coronal** (ant/post), **Transverse/Horizontal** (sup/inf). Quadrants: **RUQ** (liver/GB), **LUQ** (spleen/stomach), **RLQ** (appendix), **LLQ**, **Midline** (bladder/uterus/prostate).
- **Body Systems:** **Musculoskeletal** (33 vertebrae, 24 ribs, 6 joints, 3 muscle types; femur `1–2 L` & pelvis `~5 L` blood loss). **Respiratory** (upper ends at cricoid; diaphragm `60–70%` [`C3–C5`] + intercostals `30–40%`; adult RR `12–20`, child `18–37`, infant `30–60`). **Circulatory** (4 chambers/valves, SA → AV → His → Purkinje; O2 `97%` Hgb, CO2 `70%` bicarb). **Nervous/Endocrine** (CNS/PNS, RAS + `>=1` hemisphere = consciousness; **α1** vasoconstriction/sweat, **β1** ↑ HR/contractility, **β2** bronchodilation). **Skin** (epidermis, dermis, subQ), **GI, Renal, Repro**.
- **Lifespan & Word Parts:** **Pediatric** = `4–5 mm` pliable trachea, cricoid narrowest (`<6–8y`), shoulder padding (`<8–9y`), fontanelles (`3m` / `12–18m`), grunting, sudden hypotension after `>30%` blood loss, newborn bradycardia = initial hypoxia sign, `2–3×` metabolic rate. **Geriatric (`65+`)** = valve stenosis, hypertrophy, arteriosclerosis, orthostatic hypotension, no new alveoli `>20`, blunted chemoreceptors, presbycusis, peripheral neuropathy (silent MI), osteoporosis/kyphosis (`2/3`), `1/3` nephron loss (drug toxicity). **Terminology** = Combining form + Prefix + Suffix.

### 2. Cellular Metabolism & Perfusion Pathophysiology (Chapter 8)
- **Metabolism & Pump Failure:** **Aerobic** (O2 + mitochondria → high ATP + heat/CO2/water) vs. **Anaerobic** (no O2 → **lactic acid** + minimal ATP). Without ATP, the **Na+/K+ pump fails** → Na+ stays inside → water follows → cell swells and ruptures.
- **Respiratory Components (1–6):** Ambient air = `78%` N2 / `21%` O2 (**CO** blocks RBCs; **cyanide** blocks cells). **Boyle’s law** (`760 → 758 mmHg` inhalation, `762 mmHg` exhalation, `754 mmHg` pleural space). **Compliance** vs. **Airway resistance** (↑ by edema). `Minute = VT × f` (`6 L/min`) vs. `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`4.2 L/min`, `VD = 150 mL`); low VT needs **PPV**. **Central chemoreceptors** (CO2/pH) vs. **Peripheral** (O2 hypoxic drive in COPD); **VRG** (`2s/3s`), **DRG**, **Pontine**. Normal **V/Q < 1** (asthma = wasted perfusion; bleeding = wasted ventilation). Anemic patients read normal SpO2 while hypoxic.
- **Cardiovascular Components (7–11):** **~70 mL/kg** (`~4.9 L`); `55%` Plasma (albumin) / `45%` Formed Elements; **64% Venous** (drops first in bleeding). **Hydrostatic ("push")** causes edema; **Oncotic ("pull")** retains fluid. **`CO = HR × SV`** (`5 L/min`, **HR >160** drops CO; SV = Preload/Frank-Starling, Contractility, Afterload). **`BP = CO × SVR`**; **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%` systolic)** = early shock (**thready pulse**). **Baroreceptors** and **chemoreceptors** trigger SNS + **epinephrine/norepinephrine** to defend BP.
