# Module 1: Preparatory — Complete Deep-Dive (Chapters 1–6)
- **Chapter 1: EMS Systems, Research & Public Health**
  - **1.1 Evolution & Governance:**
    - *Origins:* Funeral-home ambulances (transport only) → Korean/Vietnam (field stabilization) & Iraq/Afghanistan (resuscitation/hemorrhage control)
    - *Timeline:* 1960s–70s CPR/AHA BLS → **1966** *White Paper* & Highway Safety Act (DOT/NHTSA) → **1973** EMS System Act → **1993** NREMT Blueprint → **1996/2000** *EMS Agendas* → **2005–2006** Core Content, Scope of Practice Model, IOM *EMS at the Crossroads* → **2014–2018** *EMS Agenda 2050*
    - *State Governance:* **Lead State EMS Agency** (planning, coordination, regulation, licensing agencies/personnel) + NHTSA's **10 Assessment Standards** + Gunderson's **6 Goals** & **12 Service Lines**
  - **1.2 Access, Models, Tiers & Centers:**
    - *911 Access:* **PSAP**, **E-911** (**ANI** phone #, **ALI** address), **EMDs**; Wireless (~70% of calls): Basic 911, Phase I (tower), Phase II (lat/long within **50–300 m**); **VoIP** (registered physical address)
    - *6 Delivery Models:* Fire Department, Municipal ("Third Service"), Private, Hospital-Based, Law Enforcement, Public Safety Officer
    - *4 Provider Levels:* **EMR** → **EMT** (pulse ox, auto BP, limited meds) → **AEMT** (advanced airways, BGL, IV/IO, select meds) → **Paramedic** (advanced assessment, field impression, invasive/drug care)
    - *10 Specialty Centers:* Trauma, Burn, Obstetrical, Pediatric, Poison, Stroke, Cardiac, Hyperbaric, Spine Injury, Psychiatric
    - *MIH-CP:* Mobile Integrated Healthcare & Community Paramedicine (underserved access, tele-triage, chronic disease/post-discharge care)
  - **1.3 EMT Roles, Medical Oversight, QI & Research:**
    - *Safety Priority #1 (Self → Crew → Patient → Bystanders):* Seat belts unless treating in back; stage until police clear violent/crime scenes; night reflective gear + trauma/infectious PPE; lift/carry up to **125 lbs**; good color vision; **ADA (1990)** reasonable accommodations; verbal + written report before leaving ED
    - *Medical Direction:* EMT = designated agent of **Medical Director**; **Indirect (Off-Line)** = Protocols & Standing Orders (e.g., chest pain O2, comfort position, 1 prescribed nitro if SBP > 90, 160–325 mg aspirin, ALS) vs. **Direct** = On-Line (radio/phone/telemedicine) & On-Scene
    - *CQI & Safety:* Non-punitive system audits (6 EMT roles); high-risk areas (handoffs, miscommunication, lifting/drops, crashes, spinal restriction); **closed-loop communication**
    - *Research & EBGs:* **4-step EBM**; **8-step Scientific Method**; **NEMSIS**, **CARES**, **NIGMS**; **8-step cyclical EBG process**
  - **1.4 Public Health & EMS Integration:**
    - *Public Health vs. Clinical Care:* Population focus *before* onset (APHA: *"Healthy people in healthy communities"*); CDC **5 arms** & **3 Core Functions / 10 Essential Services** (Assessment, Policy Development, Assurance)
    - *Impact & Prevention:* Life expectancy **47.3 yrs (1900)** → **78.4 yrs (2018)** → **77.0 yrs (2020/2022)**; state/local constitutional authority; **5 SDOH domains**; **Primary** (vaccines, seatbelts/helmets/DUI), **Secondary** (BP checks, reporting abuse, AEDs), **Tertiary** (acute emergency care)

- **Chapter 2: Workforce Safety and Wellness**
  - **2.1 Emotional Care, Grief & Stress:**
    - *Kübler-Ross 5 Stages:* Denial (*"Not me."*), Anger (*"Why me?"*), Bargaining (*"Okay, but first..."*), Depression (*"Okay, but I haven't..."*), Acceptance (*"Okay, I am not afraid."*)
    - *9 Interaction Principles & Crime Scenes:* Dignity, respect, clear facts, safe venting, no false assurances; clean postmortem blood/secretions & elevate head briefly if allowed—**except in possible crime scenes** (preserve evidence until cleared by police/ME)
    - *Stress & CISM:* **Acute**, **Delayed (PTSD)**, **Cumulative (burnout)**; 10 warning signs across 5 domains (Thinking, Psychological, Physical, Behavioral, Social); **Defusing** (`<=8 hrs`, `30–45 min`) vs. **CISD** (**24–72 hrs**, **7 phases**); 10 CISM components
  - **2.2 Scene Safety & Infection Control:**
    - *Golden Rule:* Never act outside medical responder role unless trained and authorized
    - *5 Pathogen Categories & 13 Pathogens:* Bacteria (antibiotic-treatable), Viruses (not antibiotic-treatable; EIDs = COVID-19, Ebola, Zika), Fungi, Protozoa (malaria), Helminths (hookworm); direct, indirect, airborne droplet spread
    - *Standard Precautions & Reporting:* Airborne exposure (TB) reported to DO within **24 hours**; bloodborne exposure (HIV/HBV) within **48 hours**
    - *Hand Hygiene & PPE:* **6-step CDC hand wash** (`>=20 sec` scrub) or `>=60%` alcohol sanitizer; treat all used PPE as contaminated; HEPA/N-95 for TB/COVID-19 (clean-shaven, annual fit test)
    - *Decontamination & Vaccines:* Wash soiled uniforms `>=25 min` in hot soapy water; **Cleaning** → **Disinfecting** → **Sterilization** (autoclave); never recap sharps; PPD TB screen + COVID-19, Tdap q10y, Hep B (offered free within **10 days**), annual flu, MMR (born `>=1957`), varicella
    - *Hazmat, Rescue & Violence:* Binoculars + DOT *ERG*; **ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2/3** or **207 PSV** roadway vests (`Class 1 <25 mph`, `Class 2 >=25 mph`); stage for police on violent/crime scenes
  - **2.3 Wellness & Safe Glove Removal:**
    - *6 Wellness Dimensions & 5 Fitness Components:* Cardio (**30–40 min, >=3 days/wk**; `Target HR = [((220 - Age) - RHR) × 0.70] + RHR`; 28 y/o, RHR 62 = **153 bpm**), Strength, Endurance, Flexibility, Body Composition
    - *Sleep & Mental Health:* Need **8–10 hrs/day** (half get `<6 hrs`); 5 NASEMSO fatigue guidelines (shifts **<24 hrs**); `>30%` first responders have mental health diagnosis; untreated **PTSD** = **6x** suicide risk (**1-800-273-8255**)
    - *7-Step Glove Removal:* Contaminated touches contaminated only; clean touches clean only

- **Chapter 3: Medical, Legal, and Ethical Issues**
  - **3.1 Scope, Standards, Duties & Ethics:**
    - *Scope of Practice* (state law: legal actions) vs. *Standard of Care* ("reasonable person standard": expected quality)
    - *Duty to Act (5 Dimensions):* On-duty obligation; off-duty assumed duty if stopping/responding (cannot leave until equal/higher training takes over; responsible for picked-up property); duties to patient, self, partner (report impairment), and equipment
    - *Protections & Ethics:* **Good Samaritan laws** (CA **1959**; unpaid good-faith care unless **gross negligence**); **Sovereign immunity** (government EMS only); Statute of limitations; Contributory negligence; NAEMT Code of Ethics (**1978**)
  - **3.2 Consent, Advance Directives & Refusals:**
    - *5-Step Approach & 4 Consent Types:* **Expressed** (rational adult), **Implied** (unresponsive/irrational emergency doctrine), **Minor** (`<18/21`, 6 emergent conditions, emancipated minor criteria), **Involuntary** (mentally incompetent or law enforcement custody)
    - *Advance Directives:* **Living Will** (**2 physicians** confirm terminal/comatose), **Durable Power of Attorney** (**1 physician** certifies incapacity), **DNR / DNRCC** (valid physical document required; act only in cardiac arrest), **POLST/MOLST** (`<=1 yr` prognosis, pre-arrest treatment limits)
    - *Refusals:* Medical **capacity** vs. legal **competence**; **7-step refusal protection** (assess, re-persuade, verify capacity, consult medical direction, stay safe, warning signs, thorough documentation + signed release/witness)
  - **3.3 Liability, Privacy, Federal Laws & Special Situations:**
    - *Torts & Negligence:* Criminal vs. Civil (**plaintiff** sues **defendants**); **Negligence (4 elements):** Duty to act + Breach (*simple* vs. *gross*) + Damages + Proximate cause (*res ipsa loquitur*, *negligence per se*); **5 Intentional Torts:** Abandonment, Assault, Battery, False Imprisonment, Defamation (**Slander** = spoken, **Libel** = written)
    - *HIPAA (1996) & COBRA/EMTALA:* 5 HIPAA disclosures without consent (continuing care, mandatory reporting, police investigation, billing, subpoena); EMTALA ("anti-patient-dumping") + **5 interfacility transfer safeguards**
    - *Special Situations:* Organ donors (patient first, donor last); **accidental hypothermia** exception; **5 presumptive** vs. **4 obvious** signs of death; **2022 NASEMSO** 8 biological exclusions; crime-scene evidence rules; **Baby Safe-Haven laws** (TX **1999**, 3 days to 1 yr)

- **Chapter 4: Documentation**
  - **4.1 Goals, Minimum Data Set & NEMSIS:**
    - *3 Goals & 5 Functions:* Continuity of care, legal protection, reimbursement + admin/billing/stats, research/education, CQI; *"If it wasn’t written down, it wasn’t done"* & *"If it wasn’t done, don’t write it down"*
    - *U.S. DOT Minimum Data Set:* **8 Patient Elements** (`BP >3 yrs`, `cap refill <6 yrs`) + **6 Administrative Elements** + **synchronous timekeeping**
    - *5 Core PCR Sections:* Admin info, demographics, vitals (**>=2 complete sets** with position/times), narrative (chief complaint in quotes, objective vs. pertinent subjective, **pertinent negatives**), and chronological treatments/responses
    - *2022 NASEMSO / NEMSIS v3.4:* Mandatory timestamps (**unit notified** & **back in service**); **Primary impression** = most acute problem; **Tiered vitals** (Routine → Initial → Full); aspirin **324 mg** PTA = *"medication already taken"*; procedure attempts = **"1"**; **Mandatory** vs. **Required** fields (`d` and `e` datasets)
  - **4.2 Narratives, Abbreviations, Legal & Reimbursement:**
    - *Narrative Formats:* Medical Narrative, **SOAP/SOAPIE**, **CHART/DCHART**, **CHEATED**
    - *Abbreviations:* Never invent abbreviations; writing terms out is preferred (`PE` = physical exam or pulmonary embolism)
    - *Legal & Error Correction:* **8-Step Refusal**; **Zero Falsification** (**False Claims Act**); paper before = single line + initials; paper after = single line + note + initials + date/time + notify; ePCR = timestamped amendment; **Drop reports**, **MCI triage tags**, **Special reports**
    - *Medical Necessity:* Commercial (**prudent layperson**), Medicare (**42 CFR § 410.40(d)**), Medicaid (state rules), **ET3** (CMS/HHS 2019, launched Jan 5, 2020)

- **Chapter 5: Communication**
  - **5.1 Hardware, FCC & Radio Rules:**
    - *Milestones & Specs:* **1973 EMS Act** (1 of 15 components) & **2012 FirstNet**; **Base Station** (80–150 W), **LMRS** (700–800 MHz), **Mobile** (20–50 W, 10–15 mi), **Portable** (1–5 W), **Repeaters**, **Encoder/Decoder**, **MDT**, **Cellular** (2–40 sq mi), **Telemetry**, **Telemedicine**, **Satellite**; **FCC** licensing/rules + daily maintenance
    - *11-Step Progression & 16 Radio Rules:* Wait **1 sec** after PTT, mic **2–3 in** from lips, pause at **30 sec**, plain English, no patient names, **closed-loop echo** repeat-back, *"we"*, *"affirmative/negative"*, *"Over"*
    - *Dispatch & Handoffs:* **AACN**; **9 dispatch points**; **24-hr military time**; **SBAR**; pre-transport radio report vs. in-person oral handoff; scene authority transfers
  - **5.2 Therapeutic & Nonverbal Communication:**
    - *Loop & 9 Responses:* **Sender → Encoding → Receiver → Decoding → Feedback**; Facilitation, Clarification, Summary, Explanation, Silence, Reflection, Empathy, Confrontation, Facilitated Communication
    - *Three Cs & Nonverbal:* Competence, Confidence, Compassion; open posture, eye level, **Intimate (`<1½ ft`) / Personal (`1½–4 ft`) / Social (`4–12 ft`) / Public (`>=12 ft`)**, gestures, eye contact, **haptics**
    - *Interviewing & Special Care:* **Open-ended** vs. **Closed-ended** (avoid **leading questions**); overcome **ethnocentrism**; elderly, pediatric (truth + eye level), hostile patients, **Family-Centered Care (1987)**

- **Chapter 6: Lifting and Moving Patients**
  - **6.1 Body Mechanics, Lifting & Moves:**
    - *4 Principles:* Keep weight close; engage legs/hips/glutes/abs; stack shoulders/hips/feet (never lift & twist); reduce lift height/distance; avoid **lordosis** & **kyphosis**
    - *Techniques & Limits:* **Power Lift & Power Grip** (hands `>=10 in` apart, drive through heels/arches, upper body before hips); **Squat Lift** (weak leg forward); **1-Hand Carry** (`<=45°` tilt); heavy 2-rescuer carry `<=1 min`; 100% effort `6 sec`, 50% effort `1 min`; forward reach `15–20 in`; push over pull
    - *3 Move Categories:* **Emergency** (Armpit-Forearm, Shirt, Blanket drags along long axis), **Urgent** (Rapid & Self-Extrication), **Nonurgent** (Walking assist, Direct Ground Lift, Extremity Lift, Direct Carry, Draw Sheet)
  - **6.2 Packaging, Devices, Stairs & Air Transport:**
    - *Devices & Limits:* Wheeled stretcher (**650 lbs**; manual ~70–95 lbs vs. power up to 140 lbs), Bariatric (**850–1,600 lbs**), Portable (**350 lbs**), Stair Chair (**500 lbs** powered; contraindicated in AMS, spinal, or lower-extremity injury), Backboard/Vacuum Mattress, Scoop (**300 lbs**; not for spinal injury), Stokes Basket, Reeves
    - *Carrying & Stairs:* `>=3` straps; hands secured inside; **feet first down / head first up**; stronger EMT at head + **spotter**; Diamond vs. Rectangular 4-person carry
    - *Positioning & Air Transport:* Unresponsive non-trauma = **left lateral recumbent**; shock = supine; **pregnancy `>20 wks`** = left side or right hip elevated (**supine hypotensive syndrome**); prolapsed cord = hips elevated; **Neonatal Isolette**; **13 Air-Medical Packaging Rules** (hazmat decon first, avoid tail rotor, IV bag on chest)
