# Module 1: Preparatory — 5-Mile-High TL;DR
- **1. EMS Systems & Public Health (Ch. 1)**
  - **Evolution & Governance:** 1966 White Paper & Highway Safety Act → 1973 EMS Act → 2006 Scope of Practice → Agenda 2050; State Lead EMS Agency + NHTSA 10 Standards
  - **Access & Delivery:** PSAP, E-911 (ANI/ALI), EMDs, Wireless Phase I/II (`50–300 m`), VoIP; 6 models (Fire, Third Service, Private, Hospital, LE, PSO)
  - **Tiers, Centers & MIH-CP:** EMR → EMT → AEMT → Paramedic; 10 Specialty Centers; MIH-CP (tele-triage, chronic/post-discharge care)
  - **Oversight, QI & Research:** Medical Director (Off-line Protocols/Standing Orders vs. On-line/On-scene); `125-lb` lift; ADA (1990); CQI, closed-loop communication, EBM, NEMSIS, CARES, 8-step EBG cycle
  - **Public Health:** Population focus *before* onset; CDC 3 Core Functions; life expectancy `47.3` (1900) → `78.4` (2018); 5 SDOH domains; Primary, Secondary, Tertiary prevention

- **2. Workforce Safety & Wellness (Ch. 2)**
  - **Emotional Care & CISM:** Kübler-Ross 5 stages; preserve postmortem crime scenes; Acute, Delayed (PTSD), Cumulative (burnout) stress; Defusing (`<=8h`, `30–45m`) vs. CISD (`24–72h`, 7 phases)
  - **Infection Control:** 5 pathogen groups + 13 pathogens; OSHA Standard Precautions (`24h` airborne / `48h` bloodborne reporting); `>=20s` CDC hand wash or `>=60%` sanitizer; HEPA/N-95; Cleaning → Disinfecting → Sterilization; PPD + vaccines
  - **Hazmat, Rescue & Violence:** Binoculars + DOT *ERG*; ANSI/ISEA Class 2/3 or 207 PSV vests (`Class 1 <25 mph`, `Class 2 >=25 mph`); stage for police on violent/crime scenes
  - **Wellness & Glove Removal:** Cardio `30–40 min, >=3x/wk` (`Target HR = [((220 - Age) - RHR) × 0.70] + RHR`); `8–10h` sleep (`<24h` shifts); PTSD = `6x` suicide risk (`1-800-273-8255`); 7-step glove removal (clean-to-clean, dirty-to-dirty)

- **3. Medical, Legal & Ethical Issues (Ch. 3)**
  - **Scope, Duties & Protections:** Scope of practice (legal) vs. Standard of care (expected); on/off-duty Duty to Act; Good Samaritan (CA 1959; gross negligence exception); Sovereign immunity (gov only)
  - **Consent & Directives:** Expressed, Implied, Minor (`<18/21`, 6 emergent rules, emancipated), Involuntary; Living Will (2 MDs), Durable POA (1 MD), DNR/DNRCC (cardiac arrest only), POLST (`<=1 yr`); 7-step refusal protection
  - **Torts, Privacy & Special Rules:** Negligence (4 elements: Duty + Breach + Damages + Proximate Cause); 5 Intentional Torts (Abandonment, Assault, Battery, False Imprisonment, Defamation [Slander/Libel]); HIPAA (1996, 5 disclosures); COBRA/EMTALA; Hypothermia exception; 2022 NASEMSO death criteria; crime-scene preservation; Safe-Haven laws (1999)

- **4. Documentation (Ch. 4)**
  - **Goals & Golden Rules:** Continuity of care, legal protection, reimbursement; *"If it wasn’t written down, it wasn’t done"* & *"If it wasn’t done, don’t write it down"*
  - **DOT Minimum Data Set & NEMSIS:** 8 patient elements (`BP >3 yrs`, `cap refill <6 yrs`) + 6 admin elements + synchronous clocks; `>=2` vitals sets; pertinent negatives; 2022 NASEMSO / NEMSIS v3.4 (Primary impression = most acute; Routine/Initial/Full vitals; `324 mg` aspirin PTA)
  - **Formats, Legal & Billing:** SOAP/SOAPIE, CHART/DCHART, CHEATED; never invent abbreviations; 8-step refusal; zero falsification (False Claims Act); single-line error correction; Medical necessity (Prudent Layperson, Medicare `42 CFR § 410.40(d)`, Medicaid, ET3)

- **5. Communication (Ch. 5)**
  - **Hardware, FCC & Radio Rules:** 1973 EMS Act & 2012 FirstNet; Base (`80–150 W`), Mobile (`20–50 W, 10–15 mi`), Portable (`1–5 W`), Repeaters, Encoder/Decoder, MDT, Cellular, Telemetry, Telemedicine; FCC rules; `1-sec` PTT pause, `2–3 in` mic, `30-sec` pause, closed-loop echo, no patient names, 9 dispatch points, `24-hr` military time, SBAR
  - **Therapeutic & Nonverbal:** Sender → Encoding → Receiver → Decoding → Feedback; 9 therapeutic responses; Three Cs; Distance zones (`<1½ ft` intimate, `1½–4 ft` personal, `4–12 ft` social, `>=12 ft` public); Open/Closed vs. Leading questions; overcome ethnocentrism; elderly, pediatric, hostile, Family-Centered Care (1987)

- **6. Lifting & Moving Patients (Ch. 6)**
  - **Body Mechanics & Lifting:** 4 principles (keep close, legs/glutes/abs, stack/never twist, reduce distance); avoid lordosis/kyphosis; Power Lift & Power Grip (`>=10 in` hands); Squat Lift; 1-hand carry (`<=45°`); `<=1 min` heavy carry; `15–20 in` reach; push over pull
  - **Moves & Devices:** Emergency (long-axis drags), Urgent (Rapid & Self-Extrication), Nonurgent (Ground, Extremity, Direct Carry, Draw Sheet); Wheeled (`650 lbs`), Bariatric (`850–1,600 lbs`), Portable (`350 lbs`), Stair Chair (`500 lbs` powered; no AMS/spine/leg injury), Scoop (`300 lbs`; no spine), Stokes, Reeves
  - **Stairs, Positioning & Air Transport:** `>=3` straps; **feet first down / head first up**; stronger EMT at head + spotter; left lateral recumbent (unresponsive), supine (shock), left side/right hip elevated (`>20 wks` pregnancy); Neonatal Isolette; 13 air-medical rules
