## Read

### 1. Systems, Legal Authority & Public Health (Chapters 1 & 3)
- **System & Oversight:** Modern EMS grew from the **1966 White Paper & Highway Safety Act**, **1973 EMS System Act**, and **2006 National EMS Scope of Practice Model** (**EMR → EMT → AEMT → Paramedic**). States regulate EMS via a **lead state EMS agency** (NHTSA's **10 Standards**), while EMTs practice as designated agents of a physician **Medical Director** under **off-line** (*protocols/standing orders*) and **on-line** (*radio/phone*) direction.
- **Scope vs. Standard & Duties:** **Scope of practice** (state law) defines what is *legal*; **standard of care** ("reasonable person standard") defines *expected quality*. **Duty to act** applies on duty and whenever stopping/responding off duty (cannot leave until equal/higher training assumes care). **Good Samaritan laws** (CA **1959**) protect unpaid good-faith care unless **gross negligence**; **sovereign immunity** protects government EMS only.
- **Public Health & Research:** **CQI**, **EBM**, **NEMSIS**, **CARES**, and the **8-step EBG cycle** drive science-based care. Public health protects populations *before* onset (CDC **3 Core Functions**; life expectancy **47.3 yrs [1900] → 78.4 yrs [2018]**), with EMTs monitoring **5 SDOH domains** and delivering **Primary** (vaccines, seatbelts), **Secondary** (BP checks, reporting abuse, AEDs), and **Tertiary** (acute emergency care) prevention.

### 2. Workforce Safety, Infection Control & Wellness (Chapter 2)
- **Emotional Care & CISM:** Support dying/grieving patients through **Kübler-Ross's 5 stages** (*Denial, Anger, Bargaining, Depression, Acceptance*) without false assurances; leave postmortem crime scenes undisturbed until cleared by police/ME. Manage **acute, delayed (PTSD), and cumulative (burnout)** stress via **Defusing** (`<=8 hrs`, `30–45 min`) and **CISD** (**24–72 hrs**, **7 phases**).
- **Infection Control & Scene Hazards:** Never act outside your medical responder role. Know the **5 pathogen groups** (bacteria, viruses [COVID-19, Ebola, Zika], fungi, protozoa, helminths) and **OSHA Standard Precautions** (**24-hr** airborne / **48-hr** bloodborne reporting). Perform the **6-step CDC hand wash** (`>=20 sec`) or use `>=60%` alcohol sanitizer; wear **HEPA/N-95** respirators for TB/COVID-19 (clean-shaven, annual fit test); progress from **Cleaning → Disinfecting → Sterilization** (autoclave); never recap sharps; maintain PPD TB testing and immunizations. Scout hazmat with binoculars + DOT *ERG*, wear **ANSI/ISEA Class 2/3 or 207 PSV** vests on roadways, and stage for police on violent/crime scenes.
- **Wellness & Glove Removal:** Aim for **30–40 min** cardio `>=3x/wk` (`Target HR = [((220 - Age) - RHR) × 0.70] + RHR`), **8–10 hrs** daily sleep (`<24-hr` NASEMSO shifts), and EAP/resilience support (**>30%** first responders diagnosed; PTSD = **6x** suicide risk; **1-800-273-8255**). Remove gloves **contaminated-to-contaminated, clean-to-clean** (7 steps).

### 3. Consent, Liability, Privacy & Documentation (Chapters 3 & 4)
- **Consent, Directives & Refusals:** Obtain **Expressed**, **Implied** (emergency doctrine), **Minor** (`<18/21`; 6 emergent rules; emancipated minor exceptions), or **Involuntary** consent. Honor valid **Living Wills** (2 MDs), **Durable Powers of Attorney** (1 MD), **DNR/DNRCC** orders (cardiac arrest only; physical proof required), and **POLST/MOLST** (`<=1 yr` prognosis). For refusals, verify medical **capacity** (vs. legal **competence**), re-persuade, consult medical direction, and follow the **8-step refusal documentation protocol** with signed release + witness.
- **Torts, HIPAA & EMTALA:** **Negligence** requires all **4 elements** (**Duty to act + Breach + Damages + Proximate cause**). Avoid the **5 intentional torts**: **Abandonment, Assault, Battery, False Imprisonment, and Defamation** (*Slander* = spoken; *Libel* = written). **HIPAA (1996)** restricts disclosure to 5 legal exceptions; **COBRA/EMTALA** ("anti-patient-dumping") requires stabilization and scope-matched transfer crews. Know **5 presumptive** vs. **4 obvious** signs of death, **accidental hypothermia** rewarming rules, **2022 NASEMSO** 8 biological exclusions, crime-scene evidence preservation, and **Baby Safe-Haven laws** (TX **1999**).
- **PCR Standards & Billing:** Follow the two golden rules (*"If it wasn’t written down, it wasn’t done"* / *"If it wasn’t done, don’t write it down"*). Record the **U.S. DOT Minimum Data Set** (**8 patient elements** [BP `>3 yrs`, cap refill `<6 yrs`] + **6 admin elements**) with **synchronous clocks**, **>=2 vitals sets**, **pertinent negatives**, and **NEMSIS v3.4** elements (Primary impression = most acute problem; Routine/Initial/Full vitals; `324 mg` aspirin PTA). Use **SOAP, CHART, or CHEATED** formats; never invent abbreviations (`PE`); maintain zero falsification (**False Claims Act**); correct paper errors with a **single horizontal line + initials** (plus date/time/notice post-submission); document medical necessity for **Commercial (Prudent Layperson)**, **Medicare (42 CFR § 410.40(d))**, **Medicaid**, and **ET3** (CMS/HHS 2019 / Jan 5, 2020).

### 4. Communication, Lifting & Packaging (Chapters 5 & 6)
- **EMS Radio & Handoffs:** **EMS Act of 1973** & **FirstNet (2012)**; **Base (80–150 W)**, **Mobile (20–50 W, 10–15 mi)**, **Portable (1–5 W)**, **Repeaters**, **Encoder/Decoder**, **MDT**, **Cellular**, **Telemetry**, **Telemedicine**; **FCC** oversight. Follow the **16 radio ground rules** (`1-sec` PTT pause, `2–3 in` mic, `30-sec` pause, plain English, no patient names, **closed-loop echo** repeat-back, *"we"*, *"affirmative/negative"*, *"Over"*), **9 dispatch points**, **24-hr military time**, **SBAR**, and clean scene authority transfers.
- **Therapeutic Communication:** **Sender → Encoding → Receiver → Decoding → Feedback** loop + **9 therapeutic responses** + **Three Cs** (*Competence, Confidence, Compassion*). Respect nonverbal posture and distance zones (**Intimate `<1½ ft`**, **Personal `1½–4 ft`**, **Social `4–12 ft`**, **Public `>=12 ft`**); use **open-ended** and **closed-ended** questions (no **leading questions**); overcome **ethnocentrism**; adapt for elderly, pediatric (truth + eye level), hostile, and **Family-Centered Care (1987)** calls.
- **Lifting, Moving & Packaging:** Apply the **4 principles of body mechanics** (keep close, legs/glutes/abs, stack/never twist, reduce distance); avoid **lordosis/kyphosis**; use **Power Lift & Power Grip** (`>=10 in` hands), **Squat Lift**, or **1-hand carry** (`<=45°`); keep heavy carries `<=1 min` and forward reach `15–20 in`; push over pull. Match move category (**Emergency** long-axis drags; **Urgent** Rapid/Self-Extrication; **Nonurgent** Ground, Extremity, Direct Carry, Draw Sheet) and device (**Wheeled 650 lbs, Bariatric 850–1,600 lbs, Portable 350 lbs, Stair Chair 500 lbs [no AMS/spine/leg injury], Scoop 300 lbs [no spine injury], Stokes, Reeves**). Carry `>=3` straps, **feet first down / head first up**, stronger EMT at head with **spotter**. Position unresponsive non-trauma **left lateral recumbent**, shock **supine**, **pregnancy `>20 wks`** on **left side / right hip elevated** (**supine hypotensive syndrome**), prolapsed cord with **hips elevated**, neonates in **isolette**, and follow all **13 air-medical packaging rules**.
