## Read

### 1. Governance, Law, Safety & Mechanics (Chapters 1, 2, 3 & 6)
- **Authority & Public Health (Ch. 1):** States regulate EMS via a **lead state EMS agency** (NHTSA's **10 Standards**); EMTs practice as agents of a physician **Medical Director** (**Off-Line** protocols/standing orders vs. **On-Line / On-Scene**). Four tiers (**EMR → EMT → AEMT → Paramedic**), **10 Specialty Centers**, **MIH-CP**, **CQI/EBM/NEMSIS/CARES/8-step EBG**, and **Primary/Secondary/Tertiary** public health prevention.
- **Safety & Wellness (Ch. 2):** **Safety #1 (`Self → Crew → Patient → Bystanders`)**. **Kübler-Ross's 5 stages**; **do not clean postmortem crime scenes**. **Defusing** (`<=8h`) vs. **CISD** (`24–72h`, 7 phases). **OSHA Standard Precautions** (`24h` airborne / `48h` bloodborne), `>=20s` CDC hand wash, **HEPA/N-95**, **Cleaning → Disinfecting → Sterilization**, **7-step glove removal**, **ANSI/ISEA Class 2/3 or 207 PSV** vests, stage for police. Cardio `30–40m, >=3x/wk` (`70%` target HR), `8–10h` sleep (`<24h` shifts), PTSD = `6×` suicide risk (**1-800-273-8255**).
- **Law & Ethics (Ch. 3):** **Scope of practice** (legal) vs. **Standard of care** (quality); on/off-duty **Duty to Act**; **Good Samaritan** (CA 1959); **Sovereign immunity** (gov only). **Expressed, Implied, Minor, Involuntary** consent; **Living Will (2 MDs), POA (1 MD), DNR (cardiac arrest only), POLST (`<=1y`)**. **Negligence (4 elements)**; **5 Intentional Torts** (Abandonment, Assault, Battery, False Imprisonment, Defamation [Slander/Libel]); **HIPAA** (5 disclosures); **COBRA/EMTALA**; **2022 NASEMSO** death exclusions; crime-scene preservation; **Safe-Haven** (1999).
- **Lifting & Packaging (Ch. 6):** **4 body-mechanics principles**; **Power Lift/Grip** (`>=10 in` hands); heavy carry `<=1m`; reach `15–20 in`; push over pull. **Emergency** (long-axis drags), **Urgent** (Rapid/Self-Extrication), **Nonurgent** moves. **Wheeled (`650 lbs`), Bariatric (`850–1,600`), Portable (`350`), Stair Chair (`500`, no AMS/spine/leg), Scoop (`300`, no spine)**. `>=3` straps; **feet first down / head first up**; left-lateral pregnancy `>20w`; **Neonatal Isolette**; **13 air-medical rules**.

### 2. Documentation, Communication, Anatomy & Pathophysiology (Chapters 4, 5, 7 & 8)
- **Documentation & Communication (Ch. 4 & 5):** *"If it wasn’t written down, it wasn’t done"* / *"If it wasn’t done, don’t write it down."* **DOT Minimum Data Set** (`BP >3y`, `cap refill <6y`, `>=2` vitals sets, synchronous clocks) + **NEMSIS v3.4** (`324 mg` ASA PTA, `"1"` attempt) + **SOAP/CHART/CHEATED** + single-line error correction + **Medical Necessity** (**Prudent Layperson, `42 CFR § 410.40(d)`, Medicaid, ET3**). **Radio:** `1s` PTT, `2–3 in` mic, `30s` pause, **echo repeat-back**, no patient names, **9 dispatch points**, **24-hr military time**, **SBAR**. **Therapeutic:** **9 responses**, **Three Cs**, **distance zones** (`<1½`, `1½–4`, `4–12`, `>=12 ft`), **open/closed** (no leading), overcome **ethnocentrism**, **Family-Centered Care (1987)**.
- **Anatomy, Lifespan & Terminology (Ch. 7):** **Anatomical position**, 7 positions (Recovery turn `>30m`, Fowler’s `45°–60°`, no Trendelenburg), 3 planes, 4 quadrants. **33 vertebrae**, **24 ribs**, **6 joints**, **3 muscles**, **femur (`1–2 L`) / pelvis (`~5 L`)** bleeding; upper airway ends at **cricoid**; **diaphragm (`60–70%`, `C3–C5`)**; 4 heart valves; **RAS + `>=1` hemisphere** = consciousness; **α1/β1/β2** receptors. **Pediatric** (`4–5 mm` trachea, cricoid `<6–8y`, `>30%` blood loss, newborn bradycardia, `2–3×` metabolism) vs. **Geriatric (`65+`)** (stenosis, hypertrophy, orthostatic hypotension, presbycusis, silent MI, `2/3` kyphosis, `1/3` nephrons). **Terminology** = Combining form + Prefix + Suffix.
- **Pathophysiology (Ch. 8):** **Aerobic** (high ATP) vs. **Anaerobic** (**lactic acid**) → **Na+/K+ pump failure** → cell swells & ruptures. **78% N2 / 21% O2**; **CO** vs. **Cyanide**; **Boyle’s law** (`760/758/762/754 mmHg`); **Compliance vs. Resistance (edema)**; `Minute = VT × f` (`6 L/min`) vs. `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`4.2 L/min`, `VD = 150 mL`, low VT needs **PPV**); **Central** (CO2/pH) vs. **Peripheral** (O2 hypoxic drive) chemoreceptors; **VRG (`2s/3s`)**; **V/Q `<1`**; O2 (**97–98.5%** Hgb; anemia reads normal SpO2) & CO2 (**70%** bicarb). **~70 mL/kg** (**64% venous**); **Hydrostatic ("push")** vs. **Oncotic ("pull")**; **`CO = HR × SV`** (`5 L/min`, **HR >160** drops CO); **`BP = CO × SVR`**; **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%`)**; **Baroreceptors & Chemoreceptors**.
