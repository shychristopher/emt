# EMT Course Master (Chapters 1–8) — 5-Mile-High TL;DR
- **0. The 4 Master Bridges**
  - **Clinical Perfusion Engine (Ch. 1, 6, 7, 8):** Anatomy (Ch. 7) drives 11 perfusion components (Ch. 8); positioning (Ch. 6) modifies preload & diaphragm
  - **Authority & Legal Defense (Ch. 1, 3, 4, 5):** License + Medical Director → Therapeutic Consent (Ch. 3 & 5) → Terminology & NEMSIS PCR (Ch. 4 & 7)
  - **Safety & Longevity (Ch. 1, 2, 6):** Safety #1 + Standard Precautions/PPE/CISM/Fitness (Ch. 2) + Body Mechanics (Ch. 6)
  - **Call Loop:** Dispatch → Safety/PPE → Consent → Assessment/Pathophys → Orders/SBAR → Move/Package → Handoff/PCR → Decon/CQI

- **1. EMS Systems & Public Health (Ch. 1)**
  - **Governance & Access:** 1966 White Paper/HSA → 1973 EMS Act → 2006 Scope → Agenda 2050; State Lead Agency + NHTSA 10 Standards; PSAP/E-911/Wireless Phase II (`50–300 m`); 6 Delivery Models
  - **Tiers, Oversight & Public Health:** EMR → EMT → AEMT → Paramedic; 10 Specialty Centers; MIH-CP; Medical Director (Off-line vs. On-line); `125-lb` lift; ADA (1990); CQI, EBM, NEMSIS, CARES, 8-step EBG; CDC 3 Core Functions, 5 SDOH, Primary/Secondary/Tertiary prevention

- **2. Workforce Safety & Wellness (Ch. 2)**
  - **Stress & Infection Control:** Kübler-Ross 5 stages; preserve postmortem crime scenes; Defusing (`<=8h`) vs. CISD (`24–72h`, 7 phases); 5 pathogen groups; OSHA (`24h` airborne / `48h` bloodborne); `>=20s` hand wash; HEPA/N-95; Cleaning → Disinfecting → Sterilization; 7-step glove removal
  - **Hazards & Wellness:** Binoculars + DOT *ERG*; ANSI/ISEA Class 2/3 or 207 PSV vests; stage for police; Cardio `30–40m, >=3x/wk` (`70%` target HR); `8–10h` sleep (`<24h` shifts); PTSD = `6×` suicide risk (`1-800-273-8255`)

- **3. Medical, Legal & Ethical Issues (Ch. 3)**
  - **Scope, Consent & Directives:** Scope (legal) vs. Standard (quality); on/off-duty Duty to Act; Good Samaritan (CA 1959); Sovereign immunity (gov only); Expressed, Implied, Minor (`<18/21`, 6 rules, emancipated), Involuntary; Living Will (2 MDs), POA (1 MD), DNR (cardiac arrest only), POLST (`<=1y`)
  - **Torts, Privacy & Special Rules:** Negligence (4 elements); 5 Intentional Torts (Abandonment, Assault, Battery, False Imprisonment, Defamation [Slander/Libel]); HIPAA (1996, 5 disclosures); COBRA/EMTALA; Hypothermia exception; 2022 NASEMSO death criteria; crime scenes; Safe-Haven (1999)

- **4. Documentation (Ch. 4)**
  - **Standards & Formats:** 3 Goals (Continuity, Legal, Reimbursement); 2 Golden Rules; DOT Minimum Data Set (`BP >3y`, `cap refill <6y`, `>=2` vitals sets, synchronous clocks); NEMSIS v3.4 (`324 mg` ASA PTA, `"1"` attempt); SOAP, CHART, CHEATED; never invent abbreviations
  - **Legal & Billing:** 8-step refusal; zero falsification (False Claims Act); single-line paper error correction; Drop reports/MCI triage tags/Special reports; Prudent Layperson, Medicare `42 CFR § 410.40(d)`, Medicaid, ET3

- **5. Communication (Ch. 5)**
  - **System Radio:** 1973 EMS Act & 2012 FirstNet; Base (`80–150 W`), Mobile (`20–50 W`), Portable (`1–5 W`), Repeaters, MDT, Telemetry; FCC rules; `1s` PTT, `2–3 in` mic, `30s` pause, echo repeat-back, no patient names, 9 dispatch points, `24-hr` military time, SBAR
  - **Therapeutic:** Encoding/Decoding/Feedback; 9 responses; Three Cs; Distance zones (`<1½`, `1½–4`, `4–12`, `>=12 ft`); Open/Closed vs. Leading; overcome ethnocentrism; elderly, pediatric, hostile, Family-Centered Care (1987)

- **6. Lifting & Moving Patients (Ch. 6)**
  - **Mechanics & Moves:** 4 principles (keep close, legs/abs, stack/never twist, reduce distance); Power Lift/Grip (`>=10 in` hands); heavy carry `<=1m`; reach `15–20 in`; push over pull; Emergency (long-axis drags), Urgent (Rapid/Self-Extrication), Nonurgent
  - **Devices & Positioning:** Wheeled (`650 lbs`), Bariatric (`850–1,600`), Portable (`350`), Stair Chair (`500`, no AMS/spine/leg), Scoop (`300`, no spine); `>=3` straps; **feet first down / head first up**; left-lateral pregnancy `>20w`; Neonatal Isolette; 13 air-medical rules

- **7. Anatomy, Physiology & Terminology (Ch. 7)**
  - **Blueprint & Systems:** Anatomical position; Recovery (`>30m` turn), Fowler’s (`45°–60°`), no Trendelenburg; 3 planes; 4 quadrants; 33 vertebrae, 24 ribs, 6 joints, 3 muscles, femur (`1–2 L`) & pelvis (`~5 L`) bleeding; cricoid; diaphragm (`60–70%`, `C3–C5`); 4 heart valves; RAS + `>=1` hemisphere; α1/β1/β2 receptors
  - **Lifespan & Word Parts:** Pediatric (`4–5 mm` trachea, cricoid `<6–8y`, `>30%` blood loss, newborn bradycardia, `2–3×` metabolism) vs. Geriatric (`65+`, stenosis, hypertrophy, orthostatic hypotension, presbycusis, silent MI, `2/3` kyphosis, `1/3` nephrons); Combining form + Prefix + Suffix

- **8. Pathophysiology (Ch. 8)**
  - **Metabolism & Respiratory:** Aerobic (high ATP) vs. Anaerobic (lactic acid) → Na+/K+ pump failure → cell rupture; `78%` N2 / `21%` O2; CO vs. Cyanide; Boyle’s law (`760/758/762/754 mmHg`); `Minute = VT × f` (`6 L/min`) vs. `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`4.2 L/min`, `VD = 150 mL`, low VT needs PPV); Central vs. Peripheral chemoreceptors; VRG (`2s/3s`); V/Q `<1`
  - **Cardiovascular:** `~70 mL/kg` (`64%` venous); Hydrostatic ("push") vs. Oncotic ("pull"); `CO = HR × SV` (`5 L/min`, HR `>160` drops CO; Preload/Frank-Starling, Contractility, Afterload); `BP = CO × SVR`; Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%`); Precapillary sphincters; Baroreceptors & Chemoreceptors
