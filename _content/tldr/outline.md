# EMT Course Master Summary (Chapters 1–8) — 5-Mile-High TL;DR

## The 4 Master Bridges: How Everything Connects
1. **The Clinical Perfusion Engine (Ch. 1, 6, 7, 8):** Anatomy (Ch. 7) physically drives the **11 components of perfusion** (Ch. 8) to keep cells in **aerobic metabolism** and prevent **lactic acid / Na+/K+ pump failure**. Primary assessment priorities (Ch. 1) and clinical positioning (Ch. 6 & 7) directly protect those mechanisms.
2. **Authority, Consent & Legal Defense (Ch. 1, 3, 4, 5):** State licensure and physician **Medical Direction** authorize practice (Ch. 1 & 3); **therapeutic communication** (Ch. 5) secures valid **consent/refusal** (Ch. 3); accurate **medical terminology** (Ch. 7) and **NEMSIS/DOT-compliant PCRs** (Ch. 4) protect you in court, prove **medical necessity**, and drive **CQI/EBGs** (Ch. 1).
3. **Safety, Biomechanics & Longevity (Ch. 1, 2, 6):** Personal safety #1 (`Self → Crew → Patient → Bystanders`) combines **Standard Precautions/PPE**, **staging**, **CISM**, **sleep**, and **fitness** (Ch. 2) with the **4 principles of body mechanics** and matched carrying devices (Ch. 6).
4. **The Call Loop:** Dispatch/AACN → Scene Safety/PPE → Consent/Three Cs → Assessment/Pathophysiology → Standing Orders/SBAR → Safe Move/Packaging → Radio/Oral Handoff + PCR → Decon/CISM/CQI.

## Preparatory Foundations (Chapters 1–6)

### Chapter 1: EMS Systems, Research, and Public Health
- **Evolution & Governance:** Grew from funeral-home transport via wartime trauma lessons, **1966 White Paper & Highway Safety Act**, **1973 EMS System Act**, **2006 Scope of Practice Model**, and **EMS Agenda 2050**. States regulate EMS via a **lead state EMS agency** (NHTSA's **10 Standards**).
- **Access, Models & Tiers:** **PSAP**, **E-911** (ANI/ALI), **EMDs**, **Wireless Phase II** (`50–300 m`), **VoIP**. Six delivery models (Fire, Municipal "Third Service", Private, Hospital, Law Enforcement, Public Safety Officer). Four tiers: **EMR → EMT → AEMT → Paramedic** + **10 Specialty Centers** + **MIH-CP**.
- **Oversight, QI & Public Health:** Practice as designated agent of **Medical Director** (**Off-Line** Protocols/Standing Orders vs. **On-Line / On-Scene**). Lift **125 lbs**, good color vision, **ADA (1990)**. **CQI**, **closed-loop communication**, **EBM**, **NEMSIS**, **CARES**, **8-step EBG cycle**. Public health targets populations *before* onset (CDC **3 Core Functions**; life expectancy `47.3` [1900] → `78.4` [2018]; **5 SDOH domains**; **Primary, Secondary, Tertiary** prevention).

### Chapter 2: Workforce Safety and Wellness
- **Emotional Care & CISM:** **Kübler-Ross's 5 stages** (*Denial, Anger, Bargaining, Depression, Acceptance*); **do not clean postmortem crime scenes**. Manage **acute, delayed (PTSD), and cumulative (burnout)** stress via **Defusing** (`<=8h`, `30–45m`) and **CISD** (`24–72h`, 7 phases).
- **Infection Control & Scene Hazards:** Never act outside medical responder role. **5 pathogen groups** (bacteria, viruses [COVID-19, Ebola, Zika], fungi, protozoa, helminths) + **OSHA Standard Precautions** (**24h** airborne / **48h** bloodborne reporting). **6-step CDC hand wash** (`>=20s`) or `>=60%` sanitizer; **HEPA/N-95** (clean-shaven, annual fit test); **Cleaning → Disinfecting → Sterilization** (autoclave); **7-step safe glove removal** (dirty-to-dirty, clean-to-clean). Scout hazmat with binoculars + DOT *ERG*; **ANSI/ISEA Class 2/3 or 207 PSV** vests; stage for police on violent scenes.
- **Wellness:** Cardio **30–40 min, >=3x/wk** (`Target HR = [((220 - Age) - RHR) × 0.70] + RHR`); **8–10h** sleep (`<24h` NASEMSO shifts); PTSD = **6×** suicide risk (**1-800-273-8255**).

### Chapter 3: Medical, Legal, and Ethical Issues
- **Scope, Duties & Protections:** **Scope of practice** (state law: legal) vs. **Standard of care** ("reasonable person": quality). **Duty to act** applies on duty and when stopping/responding off duty (stay until equal/higher training). **Good Samaritan laws** (CA **1959**; gross negligence exception); **sovereign immunity** (gov EMS only); **NAEMT Code of Ethics (1978)**.
- **Consent & Directives:** **Expressed**, **Implied**, **Minor** (`<18/21`, 6 emergent rules, emancipated), **Involuntary**. **Living Will** (2 MDs), **Durable POA** (1 MD), **DNR/DNRCC** (physical proof; cardiac arrest only), **POLST/MOLST** (`<=1 yr`). Verify medical **capacity** (vs. legal **competence**) and follow **7-step refusal protection**.
- **Torts, Privacy & Special Rules:** **Negligence** requires all **4 elements** (**Duty + Breach + Damages + Proximate Cause**). **5 Intentional Torts:** Abandonment, Assault, Battery, False Imprisonment, Defamation (**Slander** = spoken, **Libel** = written). **HIPAA (1996)** (5 legal disclosures); **COBRA/EMTALA** ("anti-patient-dumping", 5 transfer safeguards). **Accidental hypothermia** exception; **2022 NASEMSO** 8 biological death exclusions; crime-scene evidence rules; **Baby Safe-Haven laws** (TX **1999**).

### Chapter 4: Documentation
- **Goals & Minimum Data Set:** Serves **continuity of care, legal protection, and reimbursement** (*"If it wasn’t written down, it wasn’t done"* / *"If it wasn’t done, don’t write it down"*). **U.S. DOT Minimum Data Set:** **8 patient elements** (`BP >3 yrs`, `cap refill <6 yrs`) + **6 admin elements** + **synchronous clocks** + **>=2 vitals sets** + **pertinent negatives**.
- **NEMSIS, Narratives & Billing:** **NEMSIS v3.4** (Primary impression = most acute; Routine/Initial/Full vitals; `324 mg` ASA PTA = *"medication already taken"*; procedure attempts = `"1"`). Use **SOAP, CHART, or CHEATED**; never invent abbreviations (`PE`). **Zero falsification** (**False Claims Act**); single-line paper error correction (post-submission adds note, date/time, notify); **Medical Necessity** (**Prudent Layperson**, **Medicare 42 CFR § 410.40(d)**, **Medicaid**, **ET3**).

### Chapter 5: Communication
- **Hardware & Radio Rules:** **1973 EMS Act** & **2012 FirstNet**; **Base (80–150 W)**, **Mobile (20–50 W, 10–15 mi)**, **Portable (1–5 W)**, **Repeaters**, **Encoder/Decoder**, **MDT**, **Cellular**, **Telemetry**, **Telemedicine**; **FCC** rules. Wait **1s** after PTT, mic **2–3 in**, pause at **30s**, plain English, no patient names, **closed-loop echo** repeat-back, **9 dispatch points**, **24-hr military time**, **SBAR**, oral handoff.
- **Therapeutic & Nonverbal:** **Sender → Encoding → Receiver → Decoding → Feedback** + **9 therapeutic responses** + **Three Cs**. Respect **distance zones** (`<1½ ft` intimate, `1½–4 ft` personal, `4–12 ft` social, `>=12 ft` public); **open/closed** questions (no **leading**); overcome **ethnocentrism**; adapt for elderly, pediatric (truth + eye level), hostile, and **Family-Centered Care (1987)**.

### Chapter 6: Lifting and Moving Patients
- **Body Mechanics & Moves:** **4 principles** (weight close, legs/glutes/abs, stack/never twist, reduce distance); avoid **lordosis/kyphosis**; **Power Lift & Power Grip** (`>=10 in` hands), **Squat Lift**, **1-Hand Carry** (`<=45°`); heavy carry `<=1 min`; reach `15–20 in`; push over pull. **Emergency** (long-axis drags), **Urgent** (Rapid/Self-Extrication), **Nonurgent** (Walking, Ground, Extremity, Direct Carry, Draw Sheet).
- **Devices, Stairs & Air Medical:** Wheeled (**650 lbs**), Bariatric (**850–1,600 lbs**), Portable (**350 lbs**), Stair Chair (**500 lbs** powered; no AMS/spine/leg injury), Scoop (**300 lbs**; no spine injury), Stokes, Reeves. `>=3` straps; **feet first down / head first up**; stronger EMT at head + **spotter**; left-lateral pregnancy `>20 wks`; **Neonatal Isolette**; **13 air-medical rules**.

## Anatomy, Physiology, Medical Terminology & Pathophysiology (Chapters 7–8)

### Chapter 7: Anatomy, Physiology, and Medical Terminology
- **Positions, Planes & Quadrants:** **Anatomical position**; **Supine, Prone, Recovery** (turn `>30m`), **Fowler’s (`45°–60°`), Semi-Fowler’s (`<45°`)**, **Trendelenburg & Shock (`~12 in`)** (no longer recommended for shock). **Midsagittal, Frontal/Coronal, Transverse/Horizontal**. **RUQ** (liver/GB), **LUQ** (spleen/stomach), **RLQ** (appendix), **LLQ**, **Midline**.
- **9 Body Systems:** **33 vertebrae** (`7C/12T/5L/5S/4Cx`), **24 ribs**, **6 joints**, **3 muscle types** (**femur `1–2 L` / pelvis `~5 L`** hemorrhage); upper airway ends at **cricoid**; **diaphragm (`60–70%`, `C3–C5`)** + intercostals (`30–40%`); 4 heart chambers/valves; **RAS + `>=1` hemisphere** = consciousness; **α1** (vasoconstriction/sweat), **β1** (↑ HR/contractility), **β2** (bronchodilation); Skin (epidermis, dermis, subQ), GI, Renal, Repro.
- **Lifespan & Word Parts:** **Pediatric** (`4–5 mm` pliable trachea, cricoid `<6–8y`, shoulder padding `<8–9y`, fontanelles `3m`/`12–18m`, grunting, RR `>=60` inadequate, sudden hypotension after `>30%` blood loss, newborn bradycardia = initial hypoxia sign, `2–3×` metabolic rate) vs. **Geriatric (`65+`, >50% calls by 2030)** (stenosis, hypertrophy, arteriosclerosis, orthostatic hypotension, no new alveoli `>20`, blunted chemoreceptors, presbycusis, peripheral neuropathy [silent MI], osteoporosis/kyphosis [`2/3`], `1/3` nephron loss [drug toxicity]). **Terminology** = Combining form + Prefix + Suffix.

### Chapter 8: Pathophysiology
- **Metabolism & Na+/K+ Pump:** **Aerobic** (O2 + mitochondria → high ATP + heat/CO2/water) vs. **Anaerobic** (no O2 → **lactic acid** + minimal ATP) → **Na+/K+ pump fails** → Na+ stays in → water follows → cell swells & ruptures.
- **11 Components of Perfusion:**
  - *Respiratory (1–6):* **78.08% N2 / 20.95% O2** (`>=21%`); **CO** (blocks RBCs) vs. **Cyanide** (blocks cells); **Boyle’s law** (`760/758/762/754 mmHg`); Compliance vs. Resistance (edema); `Minute = VT × f` (`6 L/min`) vs. `Alveolar = (VT − VD) × f` (`4.2 L/min`, `VD = 150 mL`; low VT needs **PPV**); **Central** (CO2/pH) vs. **Peripheral** (O2 hypoxic drive) chemoreceptors; **VRG (`2s/3s`) / DRG / Pontine**; **V/Q < 1**; O2 (**97–98.5%** Hgb; anemia reads normal SpO2) & CO2 (**70%** bicarb).
  - *Cardiovascular (7–11):* **~70 mL/kg** (`~4.9 L`); **55% Plasma / 45% Formed Elements**; **64% Venous** (drops first); **Hydrostatic ("push")** vs. **Oncotic ("pull")**; **`CO = HR × SV`** (`5 L/min`, **HR >160** drops CO; Preload/Frank-Starling, Contractility, Afterload); **`BP = CO × SVR`**; **Narrow Pulse Pressure (`<25%`)**; **Precapillary sphincters**; **Baroreceptors & Chemoreceptors** → SNS + epinephrine/norepinephrine.
